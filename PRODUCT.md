# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is an engineer who follows the fast-moving AI ecosystem. They open the desktop app in the morning and evening to absorb new developments quickly, then occasionally use the graph to explore relationships among briefs, people, and topics.

## Product Purpose

AI Intel is a personal intelligence reader that turns a continuously updated Markdown knowledge base into an easy-to-scan desktop experience. Success means the user can understand what changed, why it matters, and what deserves deeper attention without feeling buried by the pace of the AI race.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, technical, and clear. The interface should feel like a well-organized engineering brain dump: information-rich without looking busy, calm enough for sustained reading, and equally comfortable during daytime and nighttime use.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, ornamental card grids, news-feed clutter, gratuitous animation, decorative visual effects, and overly stylized terminal aesthetics. Visual spectacle should be reserved for the relationship graph, where it serves exploration.

## Design Principles

1. Put new information first and make each reading session immediately legible.
2. Optimize briefs for calm, sustained reading before adding dashboard density.
3. Reveal technical detail where it supports judgment, not as ambient decoration.
4. Keep navigation and status information quiet until the user needs it.
5. Let the graph be the expressive exception to an otherwise restrained interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Formal compliance certification is not a project requirement. Maintain comfortable contrast in light and dark environments, visible keyboard focus, sensible semantics, reduced-motion behavior, and readable type as baseline product quality.
