AI research covers new papers, methods, and findings across machine learning: model architectures, training, evaluation, reasoning, and interpretability. This page tracks notable work as it lands.

## Notes

- **2026-06-30** OpenAI released GeneBench-Pro: 129 synthetic biology/genomics/medicine problems for judging AI agents in computational research. GPT-5.6 Sol Pro tops at 31.5% pass rate; Claude Opus 4.8 at 16%. Finding: top models fail 70% of realistic biology tasks. 10 problems open-sourced on Hugging Face. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-genebench-pro/) · [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-genebench-pro-exposes-that-top-ai-fails-real-biology-70-of-the-time)

- **2026-06-30** Anthropic launched Claude Science in beta: an AI workbench for scientists on Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise (macOS and Linux). Pre-configured with 60+ scientific databases, renders 3D protein structures and genome browser tracks, and runs a reviewer agent that checks citations and math. Early users include Novo Nordisk and Allen Institute. Anthropic funding 50 research projects at up to $30k credits each; applications open through July 15. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-ai-drug-discovery-program-claude-science.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropics-claude-science-bets-on-workflow-not-a-new-model-to-win-over-scientists/)

- **2026-06-29** Anthropic Economic Index "Cadences" report coverage spread widely: of ~9,700 surveyed Claude users, half say AI already handles 50%+ of their job tasks; 4% say Claude could do their entire role today; usage peaks late-night and weekends for high-income workers, mirroring global work rhythms. Data covers April 10 -- June 10, 2026. [Anthropic research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report) · [GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260629-anthropic-economic-report)

- **2026-06-30** Anthropic ran "The Briefing: AI for Science" livestream (10am PST) for pharma, biotech, and research institutions -- product demos and customer case studies from Claude's use in drug discovery and lab workflows. [Anthropic event](https://www.anthropic.com/events/the-briefing-ai-for-science-virtual-event)

- **2026-06-25** Anthropic accused Alibaba of the "largest known distillation attack" on Claude: 25,000 fake accounts ran 28.8 million exchanges (April 22 - June 5) to extract Claude's agentic reasoning and long-horizon task capabilities for use in Qwen training. Anthropic notified the Senate Banking Committee and White House, calling for tighter regulatory safeguards. Dwarfs the three prior cases (DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax) from February 2026 combined. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/anthropic-alibaba-distillation-campaign.html) · [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/technology/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-largest-known-distillation-attack-on-claude)
- **2026-06-19** John Jumper (AlphaFold Nobel co-winner) announced he is leaving DeepMind for Anthropic after nine years. Anthropic has been building AI-for-science infrastructure throughout 2026: wet labs, biosystem agent research, and partnerships with the Allen Institute and HHMI. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/19/john-jumper-to-leave-google-deepmind-for-anthropic.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/)
- **2026-06-18** Noam Shazeer (Transformer co-inventor "Attention Is All You Need," Gemini co-lead at Google) left Google DeepMind for OpenAI as Lead for Architecture Research. [[sama]]: "one of the people I have most wanted to work with since the very beginning of OpenAI." Google had paid ~$2.7B to bring Shazeer back from Character.AI in 2024. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/18/noam-shazeer-google-openai-characterai)
- **2026-06-22** White House EO "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security" (signed June 2) is now the framework Anthropic must satisfy to restore Fable 5: directs NSA and Treasury to build a voluntary 30-day pre-release government access process for designated "covered frontier models" by August 1, including a classified benchmarking system the NSA director controls. NSA Director Rudd's June 21 Senate testimony that Mythos 5 autonomously breached classified systems is shaping how that benchmark will be set.
