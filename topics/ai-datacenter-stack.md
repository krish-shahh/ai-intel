The physical supply chain underneath AI: compute (GPUs, AI accelerators, CPUs, DPUs), memory (HBM, DRAM), networking (scale-up, scale-out, optics), storage (NVMe flash, object storage, KV/context cache), power (substations, transformers, UPS, PDU), cooling (liquid cooling, CDUs, chillers), and building & site (land, shell, racks, security). Tracks capacity buildouts, supply constraints, pricing cycles, and the commodities (copper, power/energy, silicon) that gate them, as distinct from the model/software layer covered elsewhere in this wiki. See [[nvidia]], [[tsmc]], and the rest of `companies/` for individual tickers.
