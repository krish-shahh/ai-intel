- **2026-06-13** US Dept of Commerce issued an export control directive on June 12 ordering Anthropic to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for any foreign national. Because Anthropic cannot separate foreign nationals in real time, both models went dark globally. The government cited a narrow jailbreak (asking the model to review code and flag vulnerabilities). Anthropic complied but disputes the rationale and warns the standard would halt all frontier deployments industry-wide. Pre-IPO contracts fell ~3.7%. The shutdown landed 11 days after Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/12/anthropics-safety-warnings-may-have-just-backfired-the-government-has-pulled-the-plug-on-its-most-powerful-ai/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-disables-access-to-fable-5-and-mythos-5-to-comply-with-government-directive.html)
