Model releases tracks new frontier and open models as they ship, along with their benchmarks and capabilities. It spans Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Microsoft, and others.

## Notes

- **2026-05-28** Claude Opus 4.8 released. Leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 61.4, ahead of GPT-5.5 (60.2) and Gemini 3.1 Pro (57). SWE-bench Pro at 69.2%, computer-use (Online-Mind2Web) at 84%.
- **2026-06** Frontier model landscape: GPT-5.5 (OpenAI, April), Grok 4.3 (xAI, April), Gemini 3.1 Pro (Google, February), Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic, May).
- **2026-06-06** Claude Opus 4.8 additional benchmarks: 96.7% on USAMO 2026 (+27 pts from Opus 4.7 in one cycle), Dynamic Workflows, Effort Control, Fast Mode 3x cheaper. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-8)
- **2026-06-06** Microsoft launched 7 MAI models at Build 2026 (June 2), including MAI-Thinking-1 (35B MoE, 256K context, 53% SWE Bench Pro), all trained from scratch without OpenAI distillation. [Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/building-a-hillclimbing-machine-launching-seven-new-mai-models/)
- **2026-06-07** GPT-5.6 appeared briefly as a canary entry in OpenAI Codex internal rollout logs, then vanished — standard sign of internal testing. Polymarket prices a June 30 release at ~68–80%. No official announcement yet. [wavespeed.ai](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/gpt-5-6-canary-leak-what-we-know/)
- **2026-06-08** Apple WWDC 2026 keynote: Siri 2.0 unveiled, powered by a custom 1.2T-param Google Gemini model licensed at ~$1B/year. Ships as a standalone chatbot app in iOS 27 (September). Tim Cook's final keynote before handing off to John Ternus on Sept 1. [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/apple-unveils-gemini-powered-siri-and-ios-27-at-wwdc-2026-b757953c) · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/wwdc-2026-what-to-expect/)
- **2026-06-08** Gemini 3.5 Flash goes mandatory default in Google Enterprise; Gemini 2.5 Flash removed from the model selector, affected agents auto-migrated. [Google Cloud release notes](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/release-notes)
- **2026-06-08** MiniMax M3 launched June 1, claiming SWE-Bench Pro scores above GPT-5.5 with a 1M-token context and native multimodal computer use. Open weights were withheld at launch; targeting HuggingFace publication around June 11. Treat open-weight claim as unverified until weights ship. [MiniMax blog](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m3) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/minimax-m3-open-weight-model-with-a-million-token-context-challenges-proprietary-leaders/)
- **2026-06-07** Gemini 3.5 Pro still not GA as of today. Announced at Google I/O (May 19) with a 2M-token context window and "Deep Think" reasoning mode. Launch expected this month. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-june-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm)
- **2026-06-06** OpenAI shipped gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b under Apache 2.0: on-prem reasoning models with function calling and structured outputs, gpt-oss-120b near o4-mini on benchmarks, available on AWS Bedrock and SageMaker. [openai.com](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-oss/)
- **2026-06-09** ChatGPT "Dreaming" memory rolled out to Plus and Pro users in the US: model continuously extracts and updates facts from full chat history in the background, doubling memory capacity; internal evals show factual recall up from 67.9% to 82.8%. [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-is-rolling-out-a-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-memory/)
