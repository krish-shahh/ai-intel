[@demishassabis](https://x.com/demishassabis)

Demis Hassabis is co-founder and CEO of Google DeepMind, formed from the 2023 merger of DeepMind (which he co-founded in 2010) and Google Brain. A former child chess prodigy and video game designer, he holds a PhD in cognitive neuroscience from UCL. He shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with John Jumper for AlphaFold's protein structure prediction work, and has led DeepMind through AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and the Gemini model series. As of 2026 he continues as CEO of Google DeepMind, overseeing Gemini development and Google's broader AI research agenda.
