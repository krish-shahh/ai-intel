[@gdb](https://x.com/gdb)

Greg Brockman is a co-founder, president, and chairman of OpenAI, and previously was CTO of Stripe. He is known as the company's "builder-in-chief," focused on engineering and large-scale AI infrastructure including the Stargate data center project. He left OpenAI alongside Altman during the November 2023 leadership crisis and returned with him. As of 2026 he remains president and has taken on expanded control over OpenAI's product strategy.
