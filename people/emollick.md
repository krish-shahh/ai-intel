[@emollick](https://x.com/emollick)

Ethan Mollick is an associate professor at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, where he studies the effects of AI on work, entrepreneurship, and education. He is best known as one of the most widely read explainers of practical generative AI use, through his Substack "One Useful Thing" and his 2024 book "Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI," a New York Times bestseller. He co-directs Wharton's Generative AI Labs, which build prototypes and run research on human-AI collaboration. He was named to TIME's list of the most influential people in AI.
