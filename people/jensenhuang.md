[@jensenhuang](https://x.com/jensenhuang)

Jensen Huang is co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA, which he has led since founding it in 1993. Under his leadership NVIDIA's GPUs became the dominant hardware platform for training and running AI models, making the company one of the most valuable in the world during the generative AI boom. Known for his leather jacket and marathon keynote presentations, he has become one of the most visible figures in the AI industry, regularly commenting on compute demand and AI infrastructure buildouts. As of 2026 he continues to lead NVIDIA through its central role supplying compute for the AI race.
