[@DrJimFan](https://x.com/DrJimFan)

Jim Fan is a Director of AI and Distinguished Research Scientist at NVIDIA, where he co-founded and co-leads the GEAR group (Generalist Embodied Agent Research) with Yuke Zhu. He is best known for his work on embodied AI, robot foundation models, and humanoid robotics, including helping lead Project GR00T. Before NVIDIA he completed a PhD at Stanford under Fei-Fei Li and was OpenAI's first intern. His current focus is foundation models for general-purpose robots in virtual and physical environments.
