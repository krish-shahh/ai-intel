[@steipete](https://x.com/steipete)

Peter Steinberger is an Austrian programmer and entrepreneur who founded PSPDFKit in 2010, building it into a widely used PDF and document processing SDK before a nine-figure exit in 2024. In November 2025 he released OpenClaw, an open source autonomous AI agent that operates through messaging platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, and Discord. In February 2026 he joined OpenAI to work on personal agents, while OpenClaw moved to an independent foundation that OpenAI helps fund. His current role is at OpenAI.
