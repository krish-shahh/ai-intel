## Recent

- Testified at the Oct 5, 2026 NYC Council full-body AI safety hearing, alongside reps from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta. Said "On the current path, I think it is more likely than not that humanity loses control to these AIs, and it could end in human extinction," and accused Anthropic and OpenAI of being "extremely reckless" in the race to build more advanced AI.
