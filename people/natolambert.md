# Nathan Lambert

[@natolambert](https://x.com/natolambert)

Nathan Lambert is an AI researcher specializing in reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and post-training of language models. He spent several years at the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2) as a senior research scientist and post-training lead, where he was a core contributor to the OLMo family of fully open models as well as Tülu and RewardBench. He writes the widely read Interconnects newsletter and authored an online textbook on RLHF. He departed Ai2 in mid-2026 and, as of this writing, has not announced his next role, saying he plans to keep working on fully open post-training and medium-sized open models.
