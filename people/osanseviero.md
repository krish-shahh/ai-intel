Omar Sanseviero is an AI engineer and developer-relations leader who spent several years at Hugging Face, where he held roles including Chief Llama Officer and led platform, community, and on-device teams. He has since moved to Google DeepMind, where he leads Developer Experience across products including AI Studio, the Gemini API, and Gemma. He works at the intersection of product, engineering, research, and community to support developers building with AI. He previously worked at Google on Google Assistant and TensorFlow Graphics.
