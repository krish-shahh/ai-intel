# Dax Raad

[@thdxr](https://x.com/thdxr)

Dax Raad is a software engineer known for his work on developer tooling and serverless infrastructure, including as a core maintainer of SST, a TypeScript framework for building applications on AWS. He created OpenCode, an open source terminal-based AI coding agent that grew rapidly after its June 2025 launch into one of the most widely used open source coding tools. He is also a co-founder of Terminal, a coffee subscription service operated through a terminal interface. As of 2026 his primary work is building and maintaining OpenCode.
