[@elonmusk](https://x.com/elonmusk)

Elon Musk is the founder and CEO of xAI, the AI company he started in 2023 that builds the Grok family of large language models and chatbot. Under Musk, xAI has shipped Grok 3 (early 2025) and Grok 4 (mid 2025), built the Colossus supercomputing cluster, and integrated Grok into X. In 2026 xAI was merged into SpaceX, placing its AI work inside the same entity. Musk also runs Tesla and SpaceX and owns the social platform X.
