[@bcherny](https://x.com/bcherny)

Boris Cherny is the creator and head of Claude Code, Anthropic's command-line coding agent. Before Anthropic he spent about five years at Meta as a principal engineer and wrote the book Programming TypeScript. In mid-2025 he briefly left Anthropic for the AI coding startup Cursor (Anysphere) but returned to Anthropic within roughly two weeks. As of 2026 he leads Claude Code at Anthropic.
