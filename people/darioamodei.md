[@DarioAmodei](https://x.com/DarioAmodei)

Dario Amodei is co-founder and CEO of Anthropic, the AI safety company behind the Claude model family. He previously led research at OpenAI, including work on GPT-2 and GPT-3, before leaving in 2021 with several colleagues to found Anthropic with an explicit focus on AI safety research. He has become one of the most prominent voices on AI risk and capability timelines, publishing widely-read essays on where frontier AI is headed. As of 2026 he remains CEO of Anthropic, steering the company through its Claude releases and enterprise agent push.
