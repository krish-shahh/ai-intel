TL;DR
OpenAI's full July 9 launch package: GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna went public after a 12-day White House review, Sol runs at 750 tokens/sec on Cerebras hardware, and a new agentic product called ChatGPT Work landed alongside it. [[sama]] confirmed the model was altered "many times" during government talks.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] [[sama]] GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna are publicly live as of July 9 after the Trump administration cleared the release. Sol: $5/$30 per M tokens, flagship reasoning and long-horizon agentic work. Terra: $2.50/$15, balanced everyday model. Luna: $1/$6, fastest tier. Sol sets a new Agents' Last Exam high of 53.6 (vs Fable 5's 40.5) and hits 88.8–91.9% on Terminal-Bench 2.1; Terra at 84.3% ties Fable 5 on that bench; Luna at 82.5% beats last-gen Opus 4.8 (78.9%) at its price. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2210308/openai-rolls-out-gpt5-6-july-9/) · [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-to-release-gpt-56-sol-terra-and-luna-on-july-9/)

- [[model-releases]] Sol on Cerebras WSE-3 chips hits 750 tokens/sec — roughly 10x faster than Nvidia GPU deployments of a frontier model in production. OpenAI is limiting Cerebras access to select customers while scaling out. [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/8891f78a-c330-4652-bf49-ee1c3204e108) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-gpt-56-cerebras-inference-breakthrough/)

- [[model-releases]] GPT-5.6 is 54% more token efficient than GPT-5.5 on agentic coding tasks (per Altman on CNBC). New prompt caching with explicit cache breakpoints and 30-min minimum cache life. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/07/09/open-ai-sam-altman-chatgpt-5-6-sol.html)
