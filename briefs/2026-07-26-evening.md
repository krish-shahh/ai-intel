## Tools worth touching

### VectifyAI/PageIndex
- Does: Vectorless, reasoning-based RAG -- retrieves relevant document chunks by having a model reason about relevance rather than nearest-neighbor embedding search
- Why now: Trending on GitHub today (34.5K stars, +180 today); eliminates the embedding model dependency and index-rebuild step from a standard RAG pipeline
- Try if: Your corpus is technical docs or legal text where semantic similarity alone gives poor retrieval and you can tolerate higher per-query inference cost
- Skip if: Your corpus is large enough that per-query reasoning over chunks is prohibitively expensive, or you need sub-100ms retrieval latency
- Links: [github.com/VectifyAI/PageIndex](https://github.com/VectifyAI/PageIndex)
