## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on July 16: 2.8T-parameter MoE, 1M context, native multimodal. Debuted at #1 on Frontend Code Arena at 1679 Elo, beating Claude Fable 5 across 6 of 7 frontend domains. Open weights promised by July 27. Priced at $3/M input, $15/M output — half the cost of GPT-5.6 Sol. ## Tools & dev

- [[model-releases]] Today (July 19) is the last day of the Fable 5 free promotion. Starting July 20, Fable 5 is included in Max and Team Premium plans at 50% of weekly limits — but those limits drop by a third simultaneously. Pro and Team Standard users lose subscription access and get Fable via usage credits only ($10/M input, $50/M output), with a one-time $100 credit. [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-slashes-claude-fable-5-limits-in-max-and-team-premium-and-pushes-pro-users-toward-api-pricing/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320905/20260718/claude-fable-5-ends-subscription-limbo-permanent-max-credits-only-pro.htm)

- [[agentic-ai]] CISA's federal patch deadline for Langflow CVE-2026-55255 is today. The CVSS-9.9 IDOR flaw in /api/v1/responses lets any authenticated user invoke another user's flows and harvest embedded API keys and cloud credentials. Patch: upgrade to Langflow 1.9.1+. First AI agent framework to hit the federal must-patch list. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-orders-feds-to-prioritize-patching-langflow-auth-bypass-flaw/) · [HelpNetSecurity](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/07/08/langflow-vulnerability-cve-2026-55255-exploited/)
