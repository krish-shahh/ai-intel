TL;DR
OpenAI's 2025 financials leaked this week showing a $38.5B net loss but 40%+ gross margins on inference — training and Microsoft payments are the actual drain. ENISA met with Anthropic in San Francisco today to negotiate EU defender access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5, still blocked under the June 12 export control directive.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] OpenAI's 2025 audited financials leaked June 16 (obtained by Ed Zitron, confirmed by FT): $13B revenue, $20.9B operating loss, $38.5B net loss including a $41.5B fair-value charge from the nonprofit-to-for-profit conversion. R&D hit $19.2B; $17.2B went to Microsoft for cloud and R&D support. The numbers are ahead of IPO and make painful reading for public-market investors. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/16/openai-financials-leaked-losses-revenue-profit/) · [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/06/17/openai-s-financials-were-just-leaked-you-won-t-believe-how-much-the-company-is-losing/)

- [[model-releases]] Grok 4.3 is now GA on Amazon Bedrock (launched June 16): 1M-token context, configurable reasoning effort (none/low/medium/high), claimed lowest hallucination rate among frontier models, running on xAI's new Mantle inference engine. Priced aggressively for high-volume enterprise workloads. [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/06/grok-amazon-bedrock/) · [xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-amazon-bedrock)

## Other

- ENISA (EU cybersecurity agency) met with Anthropic in San Francisco today, pressing for a carve-out that would restore European defender access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5, blocked since the June 12 US export control directive. The meeting was pre-scheduled before the ban — making it a de facto emergency negotiation. [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/enisa-anthropic-us-ai-export-controls/) · [BanklessTimes](https://www.banklesstimes.com/articles/2026/06/18/eu-cybersecurity-agency-enisa-to-press-anthropic-on-restricted-ai-models/)
