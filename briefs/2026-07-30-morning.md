## The signal

1. **Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5 and Sonnet 5 (July 24)**
   What happened: Opus 5 launched with 1M-token context, 128K max output, $5/$25 per MTok standard (half Fable 5 price), and a 5-level effort toggle (low/medium/high/xhigh/max) spanning ~407 Elo points on GDPval-AA v2. It lands within 0.5% of Fable 5 on CursorBench 3.2, beats Fable 5 on OSWorld 2.0, and more than doubles Opus 4.8 on Frontier-Bench v0.1. Zero data retention vs. Fable 5's 30-day minimum.
   Why it matters: Near-frontier agentic and coding performance at half the Fable 5 price changes the cost-per-task math immediately; any team paying Fable 5 rates now has a direct comparison to run.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-claude-opus-5-ai-fable-5-cost.html) (independent) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5) (primary) · [Enterprise DNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/anthropic-claude-opus-5-enterprise-cost-july-2026/) (independent)

2. **Anthropic publishes formal open-weights position paper (July 27)**
   What happened: Anthropic posted a public position stating it will not open-source frontier-capable models, citing catastrophic-risk arguments. The post came the same week that 77 AI firms (Nvidia, Hugging Face, Mistral, and others -- but not OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic) signed a pro-open-weights letter urging the EU and US to protect open release rights.
   Why it matters: Anthropic has drawn a formal line between safety-justified closed weights and the broader open-source AI movement; the timing against the 77-firm letter makes it a direct political statement, not just a research position.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models) (primary) · [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/anthropic-faces-open-weights-ban-accusations-as-77-firms-sign-letter/) (community)

3. **Anthropic Mythos cracks HAWK post-quantum signature scheme in 60 hours (July 28)**
   What happened: Anthropic published research showing Claude Mythos independently identified cryptographic weaknesses and broke HAWK, a NIST-evaluated post-quantum signature scheme, in 60 hours -- a vulnerability that took human cryptographers approximately two years to find.
   Why it matters: First published case of a frontier AI autonomously defeating a post-quantum cryptographic primitive; implies that security assumptions built on NIST-evaluated post-quantum schemes may need to be revisited faster than the standards process moves.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-cryptographic-weaknesses) (primary) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/claude-ai-just-cracked-post-quantum.html) (independent)

## Models & research

### Claude Opus 5
**Claim:** 1M token context, 128K output, $5/$25 per MTok standard (fast mode $10/$50, batch $2.50/$12.50). 5-level effort toggle; billing spans ~8x token consumption between low and max. Zero data retention.
**Evidence:** CNBC and third-party coverage confirm pricing. CursorBench 3.2 within 0.5% of Fable 5; OSWorld 2.0 beats Fable 5 at ~1/3 cost; more than doubles Opus 4.8 on Frontier-Bench v0.1. Community reports noted elevated API errors July 27 and a broader Claude outage July 29 -- both appear resolved.
**Caveat:** Benchmarks are Anthropic-published; independent aggregated evals not yet out. Fast mode erases most cost advantage. "Opus 5 is a really bad model" discourse ran July 27-28 on HN and Reddit -- likely a mix of expectation mismatch and the simultaneous outage rather than a fundamental quality regression.
**Worth your time:** Read. [[simonw]] wrote a first-look July 24 if you want a practitioner sanity check. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5) · [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/24/introducing-claude-opus-5/)

### Kimi K3 (open weights, July 26)
**Claim:** 1.4 TB MXFP4 weights on Hugging Face; 2.8T-parameter MoE, 64-accelerator minimum for deployment. Custom license: free for most, but MaaS >$20M/12-month revenue requires a signed Moonshot commercial deal.
**Evidence:** Weights are live and verifiable on Hugging Face; Together AI and Modal had day-0 hosted inference. [[natolambert]] framed it as the largest open-weight release ever. Independent inference benchmarks are just starting to appear.
**Caveat:** 64-accelerator floor is a real barrier. MXFP4 needs patches in standard llama.cpp/vLLM. All published task-resolution benchmarks still come from Moonshot. License clause is easy to miss and non-trivial for API businesses.
**Worth your time:** Read. [Roo/Beehiiv](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/kimi-k3-open-weights-license-benchmarks) · [Interconnects](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k3-the-open-weights-escalation)

### Discovering Cryptographic Weaknesses with Claude (Mythos)
**Claim:** Anthropic Mythos found and exploited weaknesses in HAWK (a NIST-evaluated post-quantum signature scheme) in 60 hours autonomously.
**Evidence:** Anthropic research blog (primary source). Independent security press coverage confirms the claim and the 60-hour timeline vs. 2-year human comparison.
**Caveat:** Paper details the methodology; external cryptographers have not yet independently replicated or disputed the finding. Scope is limited to HAWK; claims about other post-quantum schemes would need separate evaluation.
**Worth your time:** Read -- if you are building anything that relies on post-quantum assumptions. [Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-cryptographic-weaknesses) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/claude-ai-just-cracked-post-quantum.html)

- Does: Anthropic's AI coding CLI; Opus 5 is now the default Opus model as of July 24

- Does: Open-source desktop AI agent built on OpenCode; explicit alternative to Claude Cowork; users supply their own API key
- Why now: Trending on GitHub July 30 with 18k+ stars; no $200/month subscription, granular folder-level permission controls
- Try if: You want Cowork-style local agent execution with full key control and no subscription
- Skip if: You need Cowork's collaboration and team features
