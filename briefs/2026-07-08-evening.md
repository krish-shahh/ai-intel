TL;DR
Anthropic's Fable 5 moved to pay-per-use credits today, ending free tier inclusion across all subscriber plans. GPT-5.6 Sol GA sits at 64% for tomorrow on Polymarket.

## Models & research

- Anthropic published "A Global Workspace in Language Models" on July 6, using a new Jacobian-lens technique to identify a small privileged neural zone in Claude called "J-space" that mirrors Global Workspace Theory of consciousness; the paper explicitly does not claim Claude is conscious. ## Tools & dev

- As of today, all Claude Fable 5 access requires prepaid usage credits at API rates ($10/M input, $50/M output) — double Opus 4.8's price and the most expensive token rate Anthropic has ever charged; the free inclusion window across all subscriber tiers ended July 7; Anthropic says it is temporary until capacity expands. [AndroidHeadlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/07/claude-fable-5-drops-subscriptions-pay-per-use-credits.html) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319864/20260707/claude-fable-5-drops-subscriptions-tonight-enable-credits-lose-access.htm)
- [[dev-tools]] Anthropic's updated privacy policy also took effect today: users whose accounts are flagged (but not banned) may be asked to submit a government ID and selfie via third-party provider Persona; applies to Free, Pro, and Max plans only, not Team or Enterprise. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/anthropic-says-claude-may-want-to-see-your-id/) · [SC Media](https://www.scworld.com/brief/anthropic-updates-privacy-policy-to-require-government-id-for-some-users)
