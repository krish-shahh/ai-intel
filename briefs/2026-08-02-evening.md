## The signal

1. DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 beats its own flagship on every agent benchmark at a fraction of the cost
   What happened: DeepSeek released the official public beta of V4-Flash-0731 on July 31; same 284B MoE architecture as the preview but re-post-trained, it now outscores V4-Pro-Preview on all nine agent and coding benchmarks published internally, including Terminal Bench 2.1 (82.7 vs. 72.1) and DeepSWE (54.4 vs. 7.3 for the Flash preview).
   Why it matters: Post-training alone flipped the value equation — the budget model beats the flagship on agentic work at $0.14/M input (roughly a third of Pro), which changes the default model choice for anyone building coding agents on DeepSeek.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322513/20260731/deepseek-retrained-v4-flash-beats-its-flagship-pro-nine-agent-benchmarks.htm) (independent) · [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/deepseek-v4-flash) (independent) · [DigitalApplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/deepseek-v4-flash-0731-official-release-agent-benchmarks) (independent)

2. EU AI Office gains fine-and-audit authority over GPAI providers today
   What happened: August 2, 2026 is the date the European Commission's enforcement powers over general-purpose AI model providers come into force; the AI Office can now demand training-data summaries and full technical documentation, commission independent model evaluations with source-code access, and issue fines up to 3% of global annual turnover or €15M.
   Why it matters: For the past year GPAI providers had legal obligations on paper with no enforcement bite; that ends today, making the EU the first jurisdiction with live authority to audit and sanction frontier models at the provider level.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714){.bias-center title="Center"} (primary) · [EU AI Act tracker](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/enforcement-of-chapter-v-under-the-eu-ai-act/) (independent)

## Models & research

### DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731
**Claim:** Official public beta of V4-Flash-0731 on July 31; same 284B MoE parameter count and architecture as the preview, improved through re-post-training on agentic, tool-use, and coding tasks.
**Evidence:** DeepSeek-published benchmarks: Terminal Bench 2.1 scored 82.7 vs. 72.1 for V4-Pro-Preview and 61.8 for the Flash Preview; DeepSWE 54.4 vs. 7.3 for the prior Flash; Toolathlon 70.3. Input price $0.14/M, output roughly one-third of Pro. [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/deepseek-v4-flash) runs third-party speed and quality corroboration.
**Caveat:** Benchmarks are self-reported by DeepSeek. The DeepSWE jump from 7.3 to 54.4 is dramatic and warrants independent reproduction before trusting for production workloads. No external audit of the post-training methodology.
**Worth your time:** Read — if you're building coding or CLI agents, this changes the cost-performance tradeoff significantly.
