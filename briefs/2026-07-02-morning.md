TL;DR
OpenAI proposes donating a 5% equity stake to the US government (Bloomberg, July 2); Claude Fable 5 returned globally July 1 after a 19-day export-control shutdown, the same day Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5 as the new default model.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30: default for Free and Pro users, $2/$10 per Mtok intro through Aug 31 (then $3/$15). New tokenizer produces ~30% more tokens per string; [[simonw]] flagged this makes it ~1.4x more expensive for English text despite the intro pricing. Agentic benchmarks: 63.2% SWE-bench (vs Opus 4.8's 69.2%), but 80.4% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 which actually beats Opus 4.8's 74.6%. 1M token context on Claude Code.
