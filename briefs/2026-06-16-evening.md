TL;DR
Fable 5 and Mythos 5 stay offline as Anthropic negotiates with the White House; Anthropic's June 15 billing split for Agent SDK usage hit developers hard yesterday.

## Models & research

- Fable 5 and Mythos 5 remain suspended globally as of June 16 — no deal announced after Anthropic's Tom Brown and Sarah Heck met White House officials over the weekend. Amazon researchers reportedly demo'd a cyberattack jailbreak on Mythos to trigger the ban; Anthropic calls it a known, minor vulnerability seen in other public models too. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-amazon-white-house) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/14/how-a-warning-from-amazon-led-the-white-house-to-shut-down-anthropics-mythos-model/) · [Anthropic statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access)

- 80+ cybersecurity executives signed an open letter June 14 asking Commerce Secretary Lutnick to lift the export controls; negotiations are still running as of today. (Polymarket: 71% yes Fable 5 access restored by July 1, active market) [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/06/us-orders-anthropic-to-suspend-fable-5.html) · [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/claude-fable-5-restored-for-us-customers-by-20260613193753196)

## Tools & dev

- Anthropic's June 15 billing split took effect yesterday: Agent SDK, `claude -p`, GitHub Actions, and third-party agents now pull from a separate monthly credit ($20 Pro / $100 Max 5x / $200 Max 20x) at full API rates with no rollover. Theo Browne (T3 Code) called it a 25x effective cost jump for heavy tool users. [devtoolpicks.com](https://devtoolpicks.com/blog/anthropic-splits-claude-subscriptions-agent-sdk-credit-june-2026) · [BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/382b1ef1c37acfb3)

- [[simonw]] wrote the community-facing breakdown of the US government Fable 5 suspension directive on June 13. [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/13/us-government-directive-to-suspend-access/)
