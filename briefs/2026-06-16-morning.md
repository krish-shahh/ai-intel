TL;DR
Anthropic flew engineers to Washington on June 15 to negotiate lifting the Fable 5 export ban and came away empty-handed. Claude Code 2.1.178 shipped the same day with new Tool(param:value) permission rules.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Anthropic sent senior engineers to DC on June 15 to try to resolve the Trump administration's export-control ban on Fable 5 and Mythos 5; CNBC and TechTimes report no deal, both sides still split on risk. The directive, invoked June 12 after a reported jailbreak, has no expiration date and bars all foreign nationals from access. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318376/20260615/anthropic-races-lift-fable-5-export-ban-top-engineers-sent-washington-deal.htm) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/15/anthropic-mythos-trump-ai.html)

- [[model-releases]] Fable 5 is free inside Claude Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise plans through June 22, then moves to usage credits at $10/M input and $50/M output (2x Opus 4.8 rates); Anthropic cites capacity as the reason for the transition. [Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/claude-fable-5-june-22-deadline)

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] v2.1.178 shipped June 15: new Tool(param:value) syntax in permission rules lets you match on tool input parameters with wildcard support (e.g. Agent(model:opus) blocks Opus subagents); nested .claude/ directories now resolve closest-wins for agents, workflows, and output-styles. Also improved auto-mode subagent classification before launch, /doctor layout overhaul, and fixes for JetBrains terminal flicker and Chrome connection drops. [Claude Code changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)
