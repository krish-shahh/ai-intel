TL;DR
Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 yesterday, the biggest model release of the year so far. Apple's WWDC brought Claude into iPhone workflows for the first time via the new Foundation Models Extensions system.

## Models & research

- Anthropic launched [[model-releases]] **Claude Fable 5** and **Claude Mythos 5** on June 9. Fable 5 is the same underlying Mythos-class model with safety classifiers attached; Mythos 5 is the raw version, restricted to vetted cyberdefense partners. Fable 5 hits 80.3% on SWE-Bench Pro vs 69.2% for Opus 4.8 and 58.6% for GPT-5.5. Priced at $10/$50 per million tokens; free for Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise through June 22. [Anthropic announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-released-claude-fable-5-its-most-powerful-model-publicly-days-after-warning-ai-is-getting-too-dangerous/)

- [[karpathy]] called Fable 5 "a major-version-bump-deserving step change forward (imo of the same order as Claude 4.5 was in November)" and noted it handles very ambitious long-horizon tasks without hand-holding. [tweet](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2064409694761054332)

## People

## Tools & dev

- At WWDC 2026 (June 8-9), Apple announced a multi-AI Extensions system for the Foundation Models framework that lets developers swap in third-party cloud models including Claude, with no session-logic changes required. Anthropic shipped a Swift package to wire Claude into the `LanguageModelSession` API on iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27. [Anthropic blog](https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-foundation-models) · [TechCrunch WWDC roundup](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/wwdc-2026-everything-announced-on-siri-ai-os-27-apple-intelligence-and-more/)

- Apple also announced Gemini-powered Siri and iOS 27 Beta 1 at the same event. The multi-AI Extensions system means devs can prototype on-device with Apple's model and route harder queries to Claude or Gemini by swapping a Swift Package Manager dependency. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/wwdc-2026-everything-announced-on-siri-ai-os-27-apple-intelligence-and-more/)
