TL;DR
Nvidia has reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, eleven days after he resigned and a week after the board chair said it "does not intend to reinstate" him. The board says the vote was unanimous and that it is now satisfied with his candor. TechCrunch adds that senior US government officials backed the move. ## The signal

1. Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia's CEO
   What happened: Nvidia announced Monday morning that its board "voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang's candor and ability to lead NVIDIA." Nvidia's newsroom headline says he's back as president, CEO, and board director. TechCrunch calls it a "stunning reversal" and says it was supported by senior officials in the US government. The collected summaries don't name those officials or say what happens to interim CEO Colette Kress or board chair Harvey C. Jones. The Nvidia announcement drew 791 points and 569 comments on Hacker News.
   Why it matters: Nvidia is the center of the AI compute stack, and its leadership has been in limbo since Nov 12. On the Nov 17 earnings call, Jones said the board did not intend to bring Huang back, so this is a full reversal in under a week. My read is that the open questions move from who runs Nvidia to what changed: the board points to "discussions and meetings," and TechCrunch points to government support, but neither summary explains it. The SEC probe, the delayed 10-Q, and the Megaspeed allegations are all still open in the records.
   Confidence: Confirmed (company announcement, reported by two independent outlets)
