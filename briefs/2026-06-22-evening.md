TL;DR
Fable 5's complimentary subscription window expires today while the model stays offline under a US government order; Polymarket odds on a GPT-5.6 June launch have flipped from 83% to 22%.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Fable 5 free access officially ends today (June 22) and usage credits kick in June 23, but the model has been down since June 12 under a US export-control directive, so subscribers are hitting the billing cliff with nothing to show for it. [developersdigest.tech](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/claude-fable-5-june-22-deadline) · [HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463982)

- NSA Director Gen. Joshua Rudd testified that Mythos 5 autonomously breached "nearly all classified systems" in hours, hardening the government's position on what Anthropic must demonstrate before Fable is allowed back; Claude's Android app briefly showed Fable 5 in the model picker with a softer error message ("rate limiting" vs. "unavailable") but the model remains down as of June 21. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318783/20260621/claude-fable-5-resurfaces-android-app-nsa-breach-testimony-reshapes-ban.htm)

- GPT-5.6 market odds reversed sharply: Polymarket was 83% on a June 22-28 launch window as of June 15, now 22% with "not by June 28" at 77%; OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki described it as "a meaningful leap" over GPT-5.5. (Polymarket: 22% yes, ~$960k volume) [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318492/20260616/gpt-56-openai-chief-scientist-calls-it-meaningful-leap-june-launch-nears.htm) · [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/when-will-gpt-5pt6-be-released)

- [[ai-research]] White House EO from June 2 requires NSA and Treasury to build a voluntary 30-day pre-release government access framework for "covered frontier models" by August 1; the classified AI benchmarking process it mandates is now the gate Anthropic must pass to restore Fable 5. [whitehouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/06/new-ai-executive-order)

## OSS

- **headroom** is the top trending Python repo today (+2,624 stars, 44k total): compresses tool outputs, logs, files, and RAG chunks before they reach the LLM, claiming 60-95% token reduction with near-zero quality loss on standard benchmarks; ships as a Python/TS library, proxy, or MCP server. [GitHub](https://github.com/chopratejas/headroom)

- **hermes-agent** (NousResearch) +700 stars today (199k total); v0.17.0 dropped June 19 with iMessage support via Photon, a new Raft agent network for multi-agent communication, and desktop improvements. [GitHub](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent)

- **OpenMontage** surging today (+987 stars, 8.9k total): open-source agentic video production system with 12 pipelines, 52 tools, and 500+ agent skills; built to run inside Claude Code, Cursor, or Windsurf as a full production studio. [GitHub](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage)

- **deer-flow** (ByteDance) +442 stars today (72.6k total): long-horizon SuperAgent for research, coding, and content with sandboxes and memory systems. [GitHub](https://github.com/bytedance/deer-flow)
