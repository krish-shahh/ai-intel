## Models & research

### Qwen 3.8 Max open weights (2.4T-A95B)
**Claim:** Alibaba released downloadable weights on August 12; 2.4T MoE with 95B active parameters, 1M-token context, native image + video input; claims 86.1 on OSWorld-Verified, ahead of Claude Fable 5 (85.0) and GPT-5.6 Sol Max (83.2).
**Evidence:** Weights confirmed on HuggingFace. OSWorld-Verified score is Alibaba's own evaluation with no independent reproduction yet.
**Caveat:** Benchmark is company-run. License terms not yet disclosed. The 27B dense companion model remains absent.
**Worth your time:** Read if you're evaluating open-weight computer-use agents; Skim otherwise.

### NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning + NeMo Switchyard
**Claim:** Released August 11; Nemotron 3.5 Lightning is a 30B MoE (3B active), Mamba-2 + MoE + Attention hybrid, 1M context, claimed 4X faster than comparably-sized models. Switchyard is an open-source LLM router that dispatches calls across models mid-task; NVIDIA reports 74% cost reduction when routing between Lightning and Claude Opus 4.8.
**Evidence:** NVIDIA official release. Customer case studies: Cognition (-28%), Ramp (-58% cost, -33% runtime). All reported by NVIDIA.
**Caveat:** Speed and cost claims are NVIDIA's own; independent benchmarks pending.
**Worth your time:** Skim if you're routing mixed-complexity workloads across open and frontier models.

## Tools worth touching

### NVIDIA NeMo Switchyard
- Does: Open-source model router that dynamically dispatches LLM calls across open and proprietary models mid-task, compatible with OpenAI and Anthropic APIs, no application rewrites required
- Why now: Just released (Aug 11), fully open source; deployed users report 28-74% cost reduction on mixed-complexity workloads
- Try if: You run workloads where a meaningful fraction of tasks don't need frontier model quality
- Skip if: Your workloads are uniformly hard, or you already route via an LLM proxy
- Links: [NVIDIA blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/route-ai-agent-workloads-across-models-with-nvidia-nemo-switchyard/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/nvidias-switchyard-router-reshuffles-ai-models-mid-task-cutting-task-costs-to-a-third-in-its-own-tests) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-releases-nemotron-3-5-lightning-nemo-switchyard-give-enterprise-ai-capability-options/)

### cathrynlavery/diagram-design
- Does: 29 self-contained HTML+SVG editorial diagram types built for Claude Code, Codex, and Pi; no external dependencies, no Mermaid, no shadows
- Why now: +4,475 stars today (14.6K total); strong practitioner signal that AI-native diagram tooling has a real gap
- Try if: You want an agent to generate publication-quality architecture or flow diagrams in a consistent visual style
