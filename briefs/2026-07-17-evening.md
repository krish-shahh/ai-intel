TL;DR
Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 landed yesterday with near-frontier agentic benchmarks and open weights coming July 27; Gemini 3.5 Pro is widely targeted for today's launch with a 2M-token context window but Google has not officially confirmed.

## Models & research

- Moonshot AI shipped **Kimi K3** (July 16): 2.8T-parameter MoE, 1M-token context, two variants (K3 Max and K3 Swarm Max). Benchmarks: 93.5% GPQA Diamond, 88.3% Terminal-Bench 2.1, 56.0% Humanity's Last Exam with tools. Beats Opus 4.8 on GDPval-AA v2 (1687 vs 1600), trails Fable 5 and Sol. Priced at $3/Mtok uncached input, $15/Mtok output. Open weights drop July 27. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems) · [kimi.com](https://www.kimi.com/blog/kimi-k3) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/moonshots-upcoming-kimi-3-is-expected-to-close-the-gap-with-anthropics-opus-4-8/)

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro** has a widely reported July 17 target today. Google rebuilt the base model from scratch after scrapping the original over SVG and recursive tool-call failures. Reported specs: 2M-token context, Deep Think reasoning mode gated behind the $250/mo Ultra tier. No official launch post or API listing confirmed as of this writing. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm)
