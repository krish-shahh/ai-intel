TL;DR
Apple sued OpenAI in federal court July 10 for trade secret theft by former employees; Google scrapped Gemini 3.5 Pro's architecture for a full July 17 restart as four DeepMind researchers defected to rivals in a single week.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Google scrapped Gemini 3.5 Pro's existing architecture and is rebuilding from scratch, with a July 17 launch target. The new version targets a 2M-token context window and a Deep Think Reasoning Layer for multi-step math and logic. Backdrop: Alphabet shed ~$225B in market cap after Noam Shazeer, John Jumper, Jonas Adler, and Alexander Pritzel all left DeepMind for rival labs in a single week. [The Agent Report](https://the-agent-report.com/2026/07/google-gemini-3-5-pro-delayed-july-2026/) · [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-launch-to-july-2026/)

- [[model-releases]] Meta launched Muse Spark 1.1 on July 10: its first closed, paid API model and its first commercial entry into agentic coding, priced at ~$1.25/$4.25 per M tokens. Distinct from the Muse Image product; positions directly against Claude Code and Codex. Community on r/LocalLLaMA is sharply critical of Meta going closed-source after years of open releases. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/10/meta-launches-muse-spark-1-1-an-agentic-ai-coding-model-with-an-api-to-challenge-openai-and-anthropic/)

- [[ai-research]] FLI Summer 2026 AI Safety Index (July 7-8): no lab scored above C+. Anthropic leads at C+; OpenAI slipped from C+ to C; Meta got D+; xAI, DeepSeek, and Mistral received F. Key finding: all four major labs have weakened or voided unilateral-pause pledges; Existential Safety was the weakest domain industry-wide. [FLI](https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/) · [Time](https://time.com/article/2026/07/07/ai-safety-rankings-openai-anthropic-meta/)

- ByteDance released Seedream 5.0 Pro (July 8): reasoning image model with deep-think prompt processing, real-time web search, interactive precision editing, layer separation to alpha PNGs, native 2K output, and on-image text in 10+ languages. Positioned as a direct rival to GPT-Image 2. [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/bytedance-seedream-5-pro-rivals-gpt-image-2/) · [Testing Catalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/bytedance-debuts-seedream-5-0-pro-with-advanced-reasoning/)

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] [[dev-tools]] Anthropic added API key expiration to Claude Console (preset, custom, or never, with email warnings ahead of expiry). Separately, Claude Code auto mode is now the default on AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry; the Bedrock integration also updated to Claude Opus 4.8. [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)

- [[agentic-ai]] VentureBeat Pulse survey (June 2026, enterprise AI teams): 50% have shipped an agent or LLM feature that passed internal evals and still caused a customer-facing failure; 66% are deploying toward or already in production without human review. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/enterprise-ai-is-entering-an-evaluation-gap-agents-are-gaining-autonomy-faster-than-companies-can-verify-them)

- FTC proposed making undisclosed AI output steering an unfair practice under Section 5: secretly designing an AI to pursue undisclosed ideological or commercial objectives while claiming "best, most accurate" results would violate federal law. Public comment open through July 31. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-seeks-public-comment-policy-statement-addressing-ai-accuracy)
