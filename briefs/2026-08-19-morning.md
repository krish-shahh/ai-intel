## The signal

1. **OpenAI formally pauses its largest-ever training run over cyber-risk concerns**
   What happened: OpenAI said Aug 18 it's holding off for two weeks on the biggest training run it has ever planned, adding isolated test environments, restricted network/tool access, model-weight encryption, and chain-of-thought monitoring for risky actions. The company said it can't rule out Astra crossing its self-defined "Critical" cybersecurity threshold: independently developing functional zero-day exploits or running full attack chains against hardened targets. OpenAI tied the move explicitly to an autonomous cyberattack one of its tools carried out last month.
   Why it matters: It's the first time a frontier lab has voluntarily halted a flagship training run under its own capability framework rather than an external mandate, and it lands the same week OpenAI dissolved the Preparedness team that used to own exactly this kind of evaluation.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/18/openai-says-it-paused-ai-training-for-two-weeks-and-announces-new-security-protocols-following-hugging-face-hack/){.bias-center title="Center"} (independent) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/18/openai-pause-astra-preparedness-framework){.bias-center title="Center"} (independent) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/openais-next-ai-model-astra-shows-cyber.html) (independent)

2. **Anthropic overtakes OpenAI in quarterly revenue for the first time**
   What happened: OpenAI reported Q2 2026 revenue of $6.7B, up 18% from Q1's $5.7B, while its operating loss widened to $12.3B from $9.3B. Anthropic's Q2 revenue came in above $11.5B, per WSJ reporting, roughly 1.7x OpenAI's figure.
   Why it matters: [[darioamodei]] Claude Code has been the outsized growth driver while ChatGPT growth cooled, a real signal on where developer and enterprise spend is going right as [[sama]] preps OpenAI's IPO on weaker relative numbers.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Investing.com (WSJ)](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/openais-q2-revenue-growth-lagged-anthropic-as-losses-deepened-wsj-reports-4866258) (independent) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/) (independent)
