## Tools & dev

- Google DevRel engineer Justin Poehnelt posted June 23 that he was fired for building the Google Workspace CLI — it hit #1 on HN, gained thousands of stars, and Google fired him; two days later Google announced an official Workspace CLI at Cloud Next. The post hit 1.1M views on X within hours. [[dev-tools]] [X thread](https://x.com/JPoehnelt/status/2069482265953087602) · [HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48649011)

- OpenAI expanded Daybreak on June 22: shipped the full GPT-5.5-Cyber (85.6% on CyberGym, vetted-access only), launched Patch the Planet with Trail of Bits to AI-patch open-source projects including curl, Python, Go, and aiohttp, and updated the Codex Security plugin. [[agentic-ai]] [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/22/openai-expands-daybreak-patch-planet-full-gpt-5-5-cyber-release/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/06/openai-expands-daybreak-with-gpt-55.html)

- [[simonw]] used Claude Code with Opus 4.8 to port the Moebius 0.2B image inpainting model entirely to the browser in one session: converted PyTorch to ONNX, pushed weights to Hugging Face, and shipped a WebGPU demo.
