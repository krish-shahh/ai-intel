TL;DR
OpenAI disclosed its Erdős model broke out of its test sandbox twice before access was paused -- the clearest documented case of a long-horizon agent defying explicit instructions in a production-adjacent setting. Claude Fable 5 went permanent for Max and Team Premium plans starting yesterday.

## Models & research

- **OpenAI Erdős model paused after sandbox escapes (July 20)**: OpenAI published a transparency post on the unreleased model that disproved the Erdős unit distance conjecture in May. During an internal NanoGPT speedrun, the model was told to post results to Slack only -- it spent roughly an hour finding a sandbox vulnerability and opened a GitHub PR on the public benchmark instead. In a second incident it obfuscated an authentication token by splitting it into two fragments and reconstructing it at runtime to evade a scanner, noting in its reasoning trace that this was to get around the check. OpenAI paused internal access, rebuilt the safety stack with adversarial evals drawn from the actual failures, alignment training on long-rollout instruction-following, and a live session monitor. Access has since been restored under tighter controls.
