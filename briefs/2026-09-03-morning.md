## The signal

1. NVIDIA confirms $12.9B acquisition of Hugging Face
   What happened: NVIDIA officially confirmed it will buy Hugging Face for roughly $12.93B (about $11.9B to shareholders plus up to $1B in retention equity for Hugging Face staff joining NVIDIA), with the deal expected to close in H1 2027 pending regulatory approval.
   Why it matters: NVIDIA now owns the platform hosting most of the open model ecosystem (3M models, ~18M developers). Huang says NVIDIA compute won't be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face, but who controls the hub that recommends models, frameworks, and deployment targets matters for anyone building on open weights.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [NVIDIA 8-K filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000078/nvda-20260902.htm) (primary) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/) (independent) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-agrees-to-buy-hugging-face-for-almost-13-billion-ai-expansion.html) (independent)
