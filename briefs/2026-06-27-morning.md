TL;DR
The US government gave Anthropic a partial win: Mythos 5 is restored for critical-infrastructure operators on day 15 of the export ban, but Fable 5 is still dark. OpenAI separately previewed GPT-5.6 Sol overnight under its own government-gated rollout.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]]: US Commerce Dept notified Anthropic overnight it can redeploy Claude Mythos 5 to US organizations operating and defending critical infrastructure; Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's letter cites "appropriate safeguards." Fable 5 remains suspended for all general API, Claude Code, and subscriber traffic. Day 15 of the ban; first permissioned restore. [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/27/2026/us-releases-powerful-anthropic-model-mythos-to-some-us-companies) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/27/commerce-anthropic-mythos-restrictions-lift) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html) · [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/trump-administration-lifts-mythos-5-blackout-clears-anthropic-model-for-100-us-partners/)

- [[model-releases]]: OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna last night in a limited rollout to ~20 government-approved partners. Sol scores 88.8% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (just ahead of Mythos 5's 88.0%) and beats GPT-5.5 on ExploitBench using roughly a third of the output tokens. Terra is pitched at 2x cheaper than Sol; Luna targets high-volume economy workloads. General availability promised in coming weeks; OpenAI said the government-gated launch "shouldn't be the norm." [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/26/openai-limits-gpt-5-6-rollout-after-government-request-says-restrictions-shouldnt-be-the-norm/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2203102/openai-starts-previewing-gpt-56-and-its-three-variants/)
