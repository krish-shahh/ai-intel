TL;DR
US government cleared Anthropic to redeploy Mythos 5 to ~100 orgs on June 27; OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 Sol the day before under similar gated restrictions.

## Models & research

- Anthropic got US government sign-off to restore Claude Mythos 5 to roughly 100 selected companies and federal agencies; Commerce Secretary Lutnick cited "significant progress" after 16 days of daily talks. Fable 5 is still blocked and has no redeployment date. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/06/27/anthropic-cleared-to-restore-mythos-5-access-to-certain-us-organisations) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html) · [AnthropicAI on X](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2070665903440871779)

- OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 as three tiered variants: Sol (frontier reasoning, long-horizon agentic), Terra (GPT-5.5-competitive at 2x lower cost), Luna (fastest/cheapest). Access is gated to "trusted partners" via API and Codex only, not in ChatGPT — same pattern as Mythos. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/openai-limits-new-ai-models-to-trusted-partners-request-us-government.html)

- China's Zhipu AI (GLM-5.2) benchmarked at or near Claude Mythos levels on security-bug detection, per Semgrep research. WSJ ran it as "China Has Matched Anthropic in Cybersecurity, Resetting AI Race" — the timing, right as US labs face export controls, is notable. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/china-zhipu-z-ai-open-source-anthropic-openai.html) · [X](https://x.com/aleabitoreddit/status/2071074680253911267)
