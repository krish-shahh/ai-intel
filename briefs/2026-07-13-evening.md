## Tools & dev

- Anthropic extended included Fable 5 access on paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, seat-based Enterprise) to July 19 at 11:59 PM PT — announced this morning via @claudeai hours after the July 12 cutoff passed. This is the second extension; the original cutoff was July 7. The 50% boost to [[claude-code]] weekly rate limits is tied to the same date. After July 19, further Fable 5 use draws from usage credits at $10/$50 per Mtok. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-stays-free-for-paid-users-until-july-19-as-anthropic-buys-more-time/) · [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/claude-fable-5-free-extension-3685103/)

- Terry Tao (Fields Medal, UCLA) posted July 11 that an AI agent ported his 27-year-old Java math course applets to JavaScript in hours and surfaced two bugs he had never found. His heuristic: use agents where failures are fast to detect, domain-visible, and bounded before they propagate. Hit the top of Hacker News July 12.
