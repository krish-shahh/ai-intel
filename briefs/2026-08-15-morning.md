## Models & research

### Qwen3.8-27B
**Claim:** Alibaba released a 27.78B dense multimodal model (text + image + video) under Apache 2.0 with a 262K native context window.
**Evidence:** Weights live on HuggingFace and ModelScope. Alibaba reports: SWE-Bench Pro 61.7%, Terminal-Bench 2.1 73.0 (vs Meta Muse Glimmer 30B at 51.7), LiveCodeBench v6 90.3%, GPQA Diamond 89.2%. All Alibaba-run benchmarks.
**Caveat:** No independent reproduction yet. CoWorkBench comparison uses an older Opus 4.6 Max baseline. Model is bigger than Muse Glimmer's 30B by a hair, which weakens the head-to-head framing.
**Worth your time:** Read if you run local coding agents; Apache 2.0 and 24GB-VRAM fit make it a practical evaluation target.

### Anthropic Model 2 (shelved)
**Claim:** The August 2026 Risk Report discloses an internal model Anthropic calls "Model 2" that is "somewhat more capable" than Mythos 5 across evaluated tasks; no external release is planned.
**Evidence:** Self-reported in Anthropic's own Risk Report. Model is not accessible outside Anthropic; no independent benchmarks exist.
**Caveat:** "Somewhat more capable" is undefined. This is a voluntary disclosure with no quantitative detail attached — treat as a signal on direction, not a benchmark.
**Worth your time:** Read (the disclosure paragraph, not the model). It updates your prior on the gap between Anthropic's frontier and what the API exposes.

### Needle 2 (Cactus Compute)
**Claim:** 45M-parameter, CQ2-bit quantized tool-calling model that ships as a 14MB binary and runs a full session in 28MB RAM on phones, wearables, and microcontrollers.
**Evidence:** 500 tok/s on Raspberry Pi 5; 400-1500 tok/s on Meta Quest 3S and Apple Vision Pro. Cactus reports it trades wins with models 5-70x larger at full f16 precision on tool-calling benchmarks. Released ~Aug 13.
**Caveat:** All benchmarks are Cactus's own. CQ2-bit baked into training limits fine-tuning paths.
**Worth your time:** Skim if you are building on-device or edge agents; otherwise skip.

## Tools worth touching

### Soup v3
- Does: Fine-tunes and post-trains LLMs from a single YAML config using layer streaming, which pages decoder layers to VRAM one at a time, letting an 8B model train at 119.6 tok/s in 3.32 GB peak VRAM on a 4GB laptop GPU
- Why now: v3 released Aug 13; supports SFT, DPO, GRPO, KTO plus eval, gating, and export in one CLI; no SSH, no distributed infra
- Try if: You want to run local fine-tuning experiments on consumer hardware without a cloud bill
- Skip if: Your target model exceeds 8B parameters or needs multi-GPU training
- Links: [GitHub](https://github.com/MakazhanAlpamys/Soup) · [product page](https://trysoup.dev/)
