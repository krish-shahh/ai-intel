TL;DR
Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 (2.8T-parameter open MoE) landed July 16 and is the biggest HN story in months (951 pts, 1,600 comments) while Bloomberg labels it a "Kimi moment" as AI stocks fell; Gemini 3.5 Pro missed its third launch deadline.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] **Kimi K3** launched July 16: 2.8T-parameter MoE with 16 of 896 experts active, 1M-token context, two variants (K3 Max for chat/agent, K3 Swarm Max for parallel processing). Benchmarks: 93.5% GPQA Diamond, beats Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5, trails only Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol. Priced $3/Mtok input, $15/Mtok output; full open weights drop July 27. Bloomberg specifically called it a "Kimi moment" with AI and semiconductor stocks falling on the news. [kimi.com](https://www.kimi.com/blog/kimi-k3) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/china-s-powerful-new-moonshot-ai-model-closes-gap-with-us-rivals) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/16/moonshots-kimi-k3-pushes-chinese-ai-into-fable-level-territory/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48935342)

- **Gemini 3.5 Pro** missed a third consecutive launch deadline as of July 16. Delays are specifically tied to coding performance gaps vs GPT-5.6; Google is reportedly weighing an interim stopgap release. No model card, pricing page, or API listing has been confirmed. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320736/20260716/rebuilt-gemini-35-pro-misses-third-deadline-google-eyes-stopgap-release.htm) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/16/gemini-3-5-pro-delays/)

- "Detecting LLM-Generated Texts with Classical ML" argues SVM and logistic regression on stylometric features outperform neural detectors for AI-text detection. 153 pts / 103 comments on HN. [blog.lyc8503.net](https://blog.lyc8503.net/en/post/llm-classifier/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48936880)

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] **v2.1.212** shipped July 17: `/fork` now copies the active conversation into a new background session (the previous in-session subagent renamed to `/subtask`); `claude auto-mode reset` added; session-wide WebSearch capped at 200 calls (tunable via `CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_WEB_SEARCHES_PER_SESSION`); subagent spawns also capped at 200 per session to stop runaway delegation chains; MCP tool calls over 2 minutes auto-move to background. Plus a symlink worktree vulnerability fix and Windows Group Policy PowerShell 5.1 fallback. [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

- **LM Studio Bionic** launched July 17: a built-in agentic layer for open models inside LM Studio, positioning it as a local-first agentic runtime. 151 pts / 62 comments on HN. [lmstudio.ai](https://lmstudio.ai/blog/introducing-lm-studio-bionic) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48939662)
