TL;DR
Fable 5/Mythos 5 access ban hits its refund cutoff today with no restoration announced; on GitHub, context-compression tool `headroom` is the day's runaway trending repo.

## Models & research

- Fable 5 and Mythos 5 remain offline. Today (June 20) is Anthropic's refund processing cutoff for customers who paid for usage between June 9 and June 14. No restoration date is confirmed; Anthropic's international chief said "coming days" on June 18, and prediction markets put the odds at roughly 57% it returns before July 1. [Fable 5 Export Ban - TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318668/20260618/fable-5-export-ban-day-six-anthropic-opens-seoul-office-vows-models-back-days.htm) · [Manifold market](https://manifold.markets/PhilipDowdell/will-anthropic-restore-access-to-fa)

- The Commerce Department has not signaled a path to lifting restrictions. All other Anthropic models (Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6, Haiku 4.5) remain fully available. [Collabnix explainer](https://collabnix.com/why-is-claude-fable-5-unavailable-the-us-export-directive-explained/)

## OSS

- `chopratejas/headroom` is today's top GitHub trending repo, gaining +4,005 stars. It compresses tool outputs, logs, and RAG chunks before they reach the LLM, claiming 60-95% token reduction with near-zero quality loss, validated on GSM8K and TruthfulQA. Ships as a library, proxy, and MCP server. [GitHub](https://github.com/chopratejas/headroom)

- `google-research/timesfm` added +1,510 stars today, resurging after Google's time-series foundation model saw new community integrations. [GitHub](https://github.com/google-research/timesfm)

- `DeusData/codebase-memory-mcp` gained +1,058 stars today. A single static C binary that indexes any codebase into a persistent knowledge graph in milliseconds, supporting 158 languages and achieving 83% answer quality at 10x fewer tokens vs file-by-file exploration. [GitHub](https://github.com/DeusData/codebase-memory-mcp)

## Other

- Context engineering is the dominant pattern driving tool momentum this week: both headroom and codebase-memory-mcp are built around shrinking what hits the model's context rather than growing the context window. The `bonigarcia/context-engineering` guide repo is also circulating. [GitHub](https://github.com/bonigarcia/context-engineering)
