TL;DR
WAIC 2026 opened in Shanghai yesterday with Xi Jinping unveiling WAICO, a 29-country China-backed AI governance body. Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8T-parameter open-weight MoE) dropped the same day, framing China's AI moment.

## Models & research

- Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on July 16 — 2.8T-parameter MoE (896 experts, 16 active per token), 1M context, native text/vision/audio. Two variants: K3 Max for chat and agents, K3 Swarm Max for parallel workloads. Open weights ship July 27; API live now at $3/$15 per million input/output tokens. Benchmarks claim it trails only Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 among all models. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/china-s-powerful-new-moonshot-ai-model-closes-gap-with-us-rivals) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems)

- Gemini 3.5 Pro missed its July 17 deadline for the third time. The rebuilt model is still failing on hallucinations and output consistency — distinct failure modes from the SVG/tool-calling bugs that drove the first rebuild. Google has reportedly registered Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash Light as stopgap names while it buys more time. Only major frontier lab without a 2026 flagship in general production. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320736/20260716/rebuilt-gemini-35-pro-misses-third-deadline-google-eyes-stopgap-release.htm) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/google-gemini-launch-delayed-as-tech-falls-short-of-internal-goals)

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] shipped a July 17 update: background sessions for /fork, smarter /resume and /background flows, new session-wide WebSearch call limit (default 200, tunable via env var), and broad fixes across Chrome, Windows, Bedrock, Vertex, hooks, and remote sessions. [GitHub releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)

- Fable 5 free access for paid Claude subscribers (Pro, Max, Team) ends July 19 at 11:59 PM PT. After the cutoff it moves to usage credits at $10/M input and $50/M output — highest published pricing for any generally available Claude model. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-stays-free-for-paid-users-until-july-19-as-anthropic-buys-more-time/)

- Microsoft Project Perception is expected to launch this month — a multi-model AI security tool using Anthropic, OpenAI, and Microsoft models with smart routing to minimize cost, positioned as a cheaper alternative to Anthropic's Mythos for vulnerability detection. [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-microsoft-project-perception-ai-security-tool/)
