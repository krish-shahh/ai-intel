## The signal

1. OpenAI names next model family "Astra," reveals it solved 10 long-open math problems
   What happened: OpenAI published a blog post on August 1 revealing an internal version of Astra -- their next model family -- solved 10 open problems across mathematics, quantum complexity, and theoretical CS, each with a machine-checkable Lean 4 certificate. Problems include the first explicit non-sofic group, a disproof of Connes's rigidity conjecture, and improved sphere-packing bounds not bettered since 1978. Altman demoed the system to senators and cabinet officials in DC this week; Astra must go through the Trump administration's planned 30-day pre-release government review before shipping.
   Why it matters: First confirmed name and capability demo for what may ship as GPT-6; if the Lean-verified math holds up under community review, it sets a new formal-reasoning bar and is the clearest signal yet that OpenAI is treating scientific reasoning as the next frontier race.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) (primary) · [[@polynoamial on X](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2083467194663571701) (primary) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-announces-its-next-major-model-astra-by-dropping-ten-previously-unsolved-math-solutions/) (independent) · [BitsMinds](https://www.bitsminds.com/news/openai-astra-ten-open-math-problems-lean-proofs-2026) (independent)
