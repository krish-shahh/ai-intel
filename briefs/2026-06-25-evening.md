OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño (June 24), a custom LLM inference chip claiming 50% cheaper inference than Nvidia GPUs. On GitHub today, OpenMontage and hermes-agent are trending hard; both are agentic OSS projects worth watching.

## Models & Research

- [[model-releases]] OpenAI + Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño (June 24): first custom LLM inference processor built in nine months design-to-tape-out, targeting ~50% lower cost per token vs Nvidia, with initial deployment slated for end of 2026. [[gdb]]: "By designing more of the stack ourselves, we can serve more intelligence with greater efficiency." ## Tools & Dev

- [[dev-tools]] Qualcomm confirmed $3.9B all-stock acquisition of Modular (Chris Lattner's AI software startup) at its June 24 Investor Day; Modular lets devs run AI across different hardware without rewrites. Qualcomm also named Meta as first customer for its C1000 data center CPUs, shipping late 2028. Tenstorrent talks ($8-10B) remain unconfirmed. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/23/anthropics-claude-tag-is-learning-your-company-one-slack-message-at-a-time/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/anthropic-wants-claude-to-be-your-new-slack-coworker)
