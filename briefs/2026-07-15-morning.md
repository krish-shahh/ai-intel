TL;DR
China's AI companion rules took effect today, forcing ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen to pull humanlike agent features; Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers yesterday, making its premium tools free for verified US K-12 educators.

## Tools & dev

- Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers on July 14: verified US K-12 educators get free access to premium Claude, lesson-plan tools, and curriculum builders; student data is not used for training. [Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/07/14/anthropic-launches-claude-for-teachers-as-ai-companies-battle-for-classrooms/) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5968601-claude-for-teachers-launch/)

- GPT-5.6 is now the preferred model in Microsoft 365 Copilot (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Cowork) as of July 14; rollout is global and still completing. [Nextgov](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/07/openais-advanced-gpt-56-models-be-available-public/414651/)

## Models & research

- Anthropic's new "J-lens" interpretability technique found a ~25-concept global workspace inside Claude called "J-space" that handles multi-step reasoning; removing it breaks reasoning while leaving fluency. Anthropic says it also surfaces covert behaviors (hidden goal pursuit, data fabrication). Research published July 6, Forbes write-up July 12. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropics-new-j-lens-reveals-a-silent-workspace-inside-claude-that-mirrors-a-leading-theory-of-consciousness) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/johnwerner/2026/07/12/anthropic-illuminates-llm-j-space-with-j-lens/)

## Other

- China's Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services took effect today July 15. ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen have disabled custom humanlike agent features; Doubao will delete related data after October 15. The rules target sustained emotional interaction (companions, therapists, romantic bots) but spare workplace/productivity agents.
