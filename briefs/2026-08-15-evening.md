## Models & research

### GLM-5.3
**Claim:** Z.ai released GLM-5.3 on August 14 via post-training on a 743B-parameter base, with no base model retrain. Claims: CyberGym 84.5 (vs. Claude Mythos 5 at 83.8 and GPT-5.6 Sol at 83.6); Terminal-Bench 3.0 28.3 (vs. prior GLM's 4.6); DeepSWE v1.1 66.9 (vs. 46.2); Agents' Last Exam 28.5 (vs. GPT-5.6 Sol at 28.3).
**Evidence:** Multiple independent outlets confirmed the launch and scores; the Terminal-Bench and CyberGym results are consistent across sources. API access is live via Z.ai. Weights are withheld for approximately two weeks pending safety hardening.
**Caveat:** All benchmarks are Z.ai-reported; no independent reproduction yet. CyberBench gains specifically include capabilities the team says "outgrew its training" — unverified claim about emergent behavior.
**Worth your time:** Read if you evaluate open-weight coding or security models; Skim otherwise.
Sources: [SiliconAngle](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/14/z-ai-debuts-glm-5-3-long-horizon-coding-cybersecurity-upgrades/) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/14/z-ai-ships-glm-5-3-without-retraining-the-base-model-better-at-complex-coding-and-long-horizon-tasks/)

### Gemini 3.7 Flash
**Claim:** Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.7 Flash on August 13. Intro pricing: $0.75 input / $3.75 output per million tokens (rises to $1.50/$7.50 January 1, 2027). Claims: Code Arena Elo 1588 (ahead of Claude Sonnet 5 at 1541 and GPT-5.6 Terra at 1523); FrontierCode 1.1 Main 43.6% (vs. 34.4% for 3.6 Flash); DeepSWE v1.1 65.3% (vs. 49.0%). 1M-token context. Multimodal input (images, video, audio, PDF).
**Evidence:** Artificial Analysis confirmed the performance profile. Available now on Gemini API, AI Studio, and Android Studio. Pricing confirmed at source.
**Caveat:** Code Arena Elo measures web-dev specifically, not general coding. Introductory price expires January 1; factor in the 2x price increase if evaluating for cost-sensitive long-term workloads.
**Worth your time:** Read if you are building coding agents or evaluating cost-performance frontiers; the price-to-capability ratio is the best in the Flash class.
Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/flash/) (primary) · [SiliconAngle](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/13/google-launches-gemini-3-7-flash-coding-ai-agent-projects/) · [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-3-7-time-frontier)
