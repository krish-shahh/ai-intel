TL;DR
The US Department of Commerce forced Anthropic to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5 globally on June 12 — the first government-mandated AI model recall on record. Moonshot AI also dropped Kimi K2.7-Code overnight, an open-weight 1T-param coding model with contested self-reported benchmarks.

## Models & research

- The US DoC issued an export control directive at 5:21 PM ET on June 12, ordering Anthropic to suspend all access to [[model-releases]] Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for any foreign national. Because Anthropic cannot separate foreign nationals in real time, it pulled both models for everyone worldwide. The directive cited a narrow jailbreak — asking the model to review a codebase and flag software vulnerabilities. Anthropic complied but pushed back publicly: it disputes that a narrow, non-universal jailbreak justifies a global recall, and warns that applying this standard industry-wide "would essentially halt all new model deployments." The shutdown came three days after launch and 11 days after Anthropic filed confidentially for an IPO; pre-IPO contracts on Hyperliquid dropped ~3.7% to ~$1,627. [Anthropic statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/12/anthropics-safety-warnings-may-have-just-backfired-the-government-has-pulled-the-plug-on-its-most-powerful-ai/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-disables-access-to-fable-5-and-mythos-5-to-comply-with-government-directive.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-disables-fable-mythos-export-controls-national-security-threat/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48511072)

- Moonshot AI open-sourced Kimi K2.7-Code on June 12: 1T-parameter MoE (32B active, 384 experts), 256K context, Modified MIT license, API at $0.95/$4.00 per M tokens. Self-reported gains are +21.8% on Kimi Code Bench v2, +31.5% on MLS Bench Lite, 30% fewer reasoning tokens vs K2.6. All three benchmarks are proprietary; no independent SWE-bench or LiveCodeBench numbers exist yet, and practitioners are calling that out. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/kimi-k2-7-code-cuts-thinking-tokens-30-practitioners-say-benchmarks-dont-check-out) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/12/moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k2-7-code-a-coding-model-reporting-21-8-on-kimi-code-bench-v2-over-k2-6/)
