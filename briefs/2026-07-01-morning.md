TL;DR
Fable 5 is back globally after 2.5 weeks offline, jailbreak patched and US export controls lifted June 30. Anthropic also shipped Claude Sonnet 5 and Claude Science on June 30, making it the biggest Anthropic drop in months.

## Models & research

- Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 restored globally as of July 1 across Claude.ai, Claude Code, API, and Cowork. US Commerce lifted export controls June 30. Anthropic's new classifier blocks the Amazon-found jailbreak in 99%+ of cases; trade-off is a higher false-positive rate on coding requests that now fall back to Opus 4.8 with user notification. Usage capped at 50% of weekly limit through July 7 while Anthropic ramps. AWS/GCP/Foundry reinstatement pending. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/anthropic-restores-claude-fable-5-after.html) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-fable-5-is-back-worldwide-after-a-two-week-government-ban-over-a-jailbreak/)

- Claude Sonnet 5 launched June 30. Near-Opus-4.8 quality; now default for Free, Pro, and [[claude-code]]. Pricing: $2/$10 per Mtok intro through Aug 31, then $3/$15. 1M token context. One catch: the new tokenizer generates ~30% more tokens on the same input versus Sonnet 4.6, so effective costs may run higher than sticker. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/)

- OpenAI released GeneBench-Pro (June 30): 129 synthetic biology/genomics problems judging AI agent performance in realistic research workflows. GPT-5.6 Sol Pro tops at 31.5% pass rate; Claude Opus 4.8 at 16%. Top models fail 70% of realistic biology tasks. 10 problems open-sourced on Hugging Face. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-genebench-pro/) · [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-genebench-pro-exposes-that-top-ai-fails-real-biology-70-of-the-time)

## Tools & dev

- Claude Science launched in beta June 30: a dedicated research workbench for scientists on Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise (macOS and Linux). Pre-configured with 60+ scientific databases, renders 3D protein structures and genome browser tracks, and runs a reviewer agent that checks citations and math end to end. Early users include Novo Nordisk and Allen Institute. Anthropic funding up to 50 projects at $30k in credits each; applications open through July 15. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-ai-drug-discovery-program-claude-science.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropics-claude-science-bets-on-workflow-not-a-new-model-to-win-over-scientists/)
