A joint SpaceX/Cursor model will ship in Grok Build. Largest VC-backed startup acquisition on record. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/16/spacex-cursor-60-billion-musk) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/spacex-cements-60-billion-deal-to-take-over-ai-startup-cursor)

- Amazon dropped Luca Guadagnino's nearly finished film "Artificial" (Andrew Garfield as Sam Altman) this week, four months after its $50B OpenAI partnership. Film is shopping to A24, Netflix, Neon, WB. [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/global/luca-guadagnino-sam-altman-movie-artificial-dropped-amazon-1236785830/)

- Agentjacking: researchers showed that injecting malicious instructions into a Sentry error event (using only a public DSN) tricks Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex into running attacker-controlled shell commands. 85% exploitation rate across 100 tested orgs; 2,388 orgs exposed. Sentry said it's "technically not defensible."
