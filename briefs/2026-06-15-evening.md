TL;DR
Anthropic's June 15 double-hit landed today: Agent SDK credits split from subscriptions and Claude Opus 4 / Sonnet 4 retired from the API. [[natolambert]] surfaced as Global CTO of AI at the Linux Foundation after his Ai2 exit.

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] Agent SDK billing split is live as of today: `claude -p`, Claude Code GitHub Actions, and third-party agents now draw from a separate monthly credit ($20 Pro / $100 Max 5x / $200 Max 20x) at full API rates with no rollover; once that credit is gone, requests stop or continue at pay-as-you-go. [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-agent-sdk-credits/) · [codersera breakdown](https://codersera.com/blog/anthropic-june-2026-billing-change-claude-code/)

- [[model-releases]] Claude Opus 4 (`claude-opus-4-20250514`) and Claude Sonnet 4 (`claude-sonnet-4-20250514`) retired from the Claude API at 9AM PT today; calls to those model IDs now return 404. Migrate to Sonnet 4.6 or Opus 4.7. [Enterprise DNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/anthropic-claude-june-15-retirements-billing-2026/) · [digg](https://digg.com/ai/g7bm7k8j)

## People

- [[natolambert]] stepped into the role of Global CTO of AI at the Linux Foundation after leaving Ai2 in early June; will focus on open model governance and fully open post-training work. [Interconnects farewell post](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/farewell-ai2)
