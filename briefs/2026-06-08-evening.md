TL;DR
Apple's WWDC 2026 keynote today — Tim Cook's last — unveiled Gemini-powered Siri 2.0 and an iOS 27 Extensions system that puts Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini on equal footing on the iPhone. Also this week: Anthropic splits its billing for agent usage starting June 15, and [[karpathy]] joined Anthropic (May 19, first brief to catch it).

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Apple unveiled Siri 2.0 at WWDC 2026 today, built on a custom 1.2T-param Google Gemini model licensed at ~$1B/year; the new standalone Siri app ships with iOS 27 in September, with Tim Cook delivering his final keynote as CEO before handing off to John Ternus on Sept 1. [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/apple-unveils-gemini-powered-siri-and-ios-27-at-wwdc-2026-b757953c) · [MacRumors roundup](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/wwdc-2026-what-to-expect/)
- Gemini 3.5 Pro has not shipped as of today; still in limited Vertex preview, no confirmed date, market bets spread across mid-to-late June; expected 2M-token context and Deep Think reasoning. [TechTimes (June 6)](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-june-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm) · [Google I/O post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/)
- MiniMax M3 launched June 1 claiming SWE-Bench Pro scores above GPT-5.5 with a 1M-token context and native multimodal computer use; weights were not released at launch, targeting HuggingFace publication around June 11 — treat the open-weight claim as unverified until then. [MiniMax blog](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m3) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/minimax-m3-open-weight-model-with-a-million-token-context-challenges-proprietary-leaders/)

## Tools & dev

- iOS 27 introduces Apple Intelligence Extensions: a three-step App Store process lets users route Siri queries to Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT instead of the default, ending ChatGPT's exclusive first-party slot it held since iOS 18.2. [MacRumors (May 5)](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/05/ios-27-third-party-chatbots-apple-intelligence/) · [Enterprise DNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/apple-wwdc-2026-gemini-siri-ios27-enterprise-2026/)
- OpenAI put Apple on legal notice in May over ChatGPT being buried inside Siri rather than featured prominently; the WWDC Extensions announcement moved ChatGPT to parity with competitors rather than restoring exclusivity. [Memeburn](https://memeburn.com/openai-legal-options-against-apple-the-2026-ai-fallout/)
- [[claude-code]] [[agentic-ai]] Claude Code shipped dynamic workflows on May 28 alongside Opus 4.8, allowing up to 1,000 parallel subagents via auto-generated JS orchestration scripts; `/effort ultracode` sets xhigh effort and auto-spawns workflows for large tasks. Research preview on Max/Team/Enterprise. [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/06/dynamic-workflows-claude-code/) · [Anthropic blog](https://claude.com/blog/introducing-dynamic-workflows-in-claude-code)
- [[claude-code]] Starting June 15, Agent SDK usage, `claude -p`, and Claude Code GitHub Actions move off subscription limits into a separate monthly credit pool ($20 Pro / $100 Max 5x / $200 Max 20x) billed at full API rates with no rollover; interactive Claude Code in the terminal is unaffected. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317625/20260602/anthropic-ends-subscription-subsidy-agents-june-15-credit-pool-replaces-flat-rate-access.htm) · [Codersera](https://codersera.com/blog/anthropic-june-2026-billing-change-claude-code/)
