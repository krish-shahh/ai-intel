TL;DR
Anthropic shipped [[model-releases|Claude Sonnet 5]] on June 30 as its cheapest capable agent model, closing the gap with Opus 4.8 and immediately becoming the free/Pro plan default. California signed the first state-wide government Claude deal the same day.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases|Claude Sonnet 5]] launched June 30: SWE-bench Pro 63.2% (up from Sonnet 4.6's 58.1%), OSWorld-Verified 81.2%, GDPval-AA v2 at 1,618 (edges Opus 4.8's 1,615 on knowledge work). Intro pricing $2/$10 per million tokens through Aug 31, then $3/$15. 1M context window. Now the default for free and Pro plans and positioned as the cost-effective path for [[agentic-ai|agentic]] workloads. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)

## Tools & dev

- California Gov. Newsom partnered with Anthropic (June 29): Claude now available to all state agencies plus cities and counties at 50% discount through the new SITeS portal. First state-government-wide Claude deployment. Claude already in use at DMV and DHCS. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/anthropic-and-gov-newsom-forge-deal-allowing-california-government-to-use-claude-at-half-price/) · [gov.ca.gov](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/06/29/governor-newsom-announces-a-first-of-its-kind-partnership-providing-anthropic-tools-to-state-agencies-and-improving-services-for-californians/)
- Cursor launched a native iOS public beta (June 29): remote-controls cloud agents or local desktop agents from phone, with voice input, slash commands, Live Activities on lock screen, and diff review. 75% off Composer 2.5 runs in the app through July 5.
