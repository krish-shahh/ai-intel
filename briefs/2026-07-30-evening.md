## The signal

1. **"Pacing the Frontier": 1,178 AI lab employees call for US-backed pacing mechanism; OpenAI and Anthropic formally endorse**
   What happened: On July 28, 1,178 verified employees across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and several smaller labs published "Pacing the Frontier" -- asking the US government to develop the technical and governance tools needed to deliberately pace automated AI development at some future point, not now. [[darioamodei]] and several Anthropic co-founders signed; OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki signed; Meta's VP of AI research signed. Both OpenAI and Anthropic formally endorsed on July 29. Mark Zuckerberg published a same-day WSJ op-ed opposing it, framing the danger as AI concentration rather than capability.
   Why it matters: For the first time, sitting CEOs and chief scientists at both US frontier labs are publicly aligned on a specific future control posture, and both companies are on record backing US-led pacing infrastructure directly ahead of the August 1 TRAINS deadline.
   Confidence: Confirmed
