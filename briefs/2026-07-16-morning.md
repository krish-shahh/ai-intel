TL;DR
Anthropic launched Ode, a $1.5B enterprise AI services firm with Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman, and confirmed IPO investor meetings are underway targeting October; Thinking Machines Lab (Mira Murati's startup) released Inkling, its first open-weight model.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling on July 15: a 975B-parameter / 41B-active MoE open-weight model trained on 45T tokens across text, image, and audio with a 1M-token context window. Available for download and fine-tuning via the company's Tinker platform. Murati's explicit bet is enterprise customizability over raw benchmark supremacy. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/thinking-machines-amps-up-its-bet-against-one-size-fits-all-ai-with-its-first-open-model-inkling/) · [Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling/)

- NVIDIA revealed Cosmos 3 Edge on July 16 during Jensen Huang's Tokyo visit: a new real-time world model for physical AI and robotics. Alongside it, Japan's government and major industrials (Toyota, Kawasaki, Fujitsu, Hitachi, Fanuc, Yaskawa) announced a national AI infrastructure with 13,750 Vera CPUs and 27,500 Rubin GPUs. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/nvidia-reveals-new-ai-model-and-expands-japans-physical-ai-ecosystem.html) · [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/japan-ecosystem-2026/)

## Tools & dev

- [[agentic-ai]] Anthropic, Blackstone, and Hellman & Friedman officially launched Ode on July 15: a $1.5B standalone AI implementation company that embeds small teams of Anthropic Applied AI engineers inside enterprise clients. Built on the Fractional AI acquisition (May 2026); Goldman Sachs, General Atlantic, Apollo, GIC, and Sequoia also invested. [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260715205134/en/Anthropic-Blackstone-and-Hellman-Friedman-Introduce-Ode-with-Anthropic-an-Enterprise-AI-Services-Firm) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/anthropic-blackstone-bet-the-next-trillion-dollar-ai-business-is-implementation-not-models/)

- Anthropic's bankers are scheduling meetings with prospective public-market investors, confirmed July 15 by CNBC and Bloomberg; listing is expected as early as October 2026, which would put Anthropic ahead of OpenAI to public markets. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/anthropic-ipo-banks-investor-meetings.html)

- Apple sued OpenAI on July 16 alleging two former engineers (Chang Liu and Tang Yew Tan) took proprietary hardware files to OpenAI; OpenAI says it has found no evidence supporting the claim. [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/16/apple-sues-openai-over-theft-of-trade-secrets/)

- Meta's custom AI chip "Iris" entered production July 15: manufacturing begins in September as part of Meta's plan to reach 14 GW of AI compute. Completed testing without major issues. [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/07/15/metas-custom-ai-chip-could-redefine-the-economics-of-ai/)

Capable reasoning without datacenter hardware. [PrismML](https://prismml.com/news/prismml-releases-bonsai-27b) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48910545)

Point release stabilizing the v0.32.0 interactive agent. [GitHub](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/releases)

- **comfy-org/ComfyUI v0.28.0** released July 15: SeedVR2 native image/video upscaling, int4 model support with Turing int8/int4 optimizations, new Save 3D / Save Splat / Save Point Cloud nodes, 4 CVE fixes. [GitHub](https://github.com/comfy-org/ComfyUI/releases)
