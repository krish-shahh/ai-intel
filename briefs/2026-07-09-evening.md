TL;DR
GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna launched publicly today after a US government safety hold. Sol's Ultra mode spawns cooperative subagents — a meaningful architectural shift for agentic work.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna went public today after the Dept. of Commerce cleared the series; they were gated since late June. Sol ($5/$30 per M tokens) is the flagship tuned for bio/chem/cybersec; Terra ($2.50/$15) is billed as 2x cheaper than GPT-5.5 at comparable quality; Luna ($1/$6) targets speed/cost. [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-to-release-gpt-56-sol-terra-and-luna-on-july-9/) · [OpenAI preview](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)

- [[agentic-ai]] Sol's Ultra mode decomposes tasks and spawns multiple cooperative subagents that communicate mid-run, not just at merge time. Terminal-Bench 2.1: 88.8% standard, 91.9% Ultra. Each Ultra call can cost several times a standard request. [apidog](https://apidog.com/blog/gpt-5-6-ultra-mode/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319802/20260706/gpt-56-release-nears-ultra-mode-spawns-subagents-terra-cuts-cost-metr-flags-risk.htm)

- [[model-releases]] Cerebras will run GPT-5.6 Sol at up to 750 tokens/sec in July — roughly 10x faster than a standard H100 cluster — initially to select customers. The Cerebras contract with OpenAI is reportedly worth $20B+ over multiple years. [Value Add VC](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/cerebras-openai-gpt-5-6-sol-750-tokens-2026)

- Polymarket signal: "Which company has best AI model end of July?" currently sitting at 87% Anthropic despite today's GPT-5.6 launch — a notable crowd read given OpenAI is shipping its strongest series. [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/which-company-has-best-ai-model-end-of-2026)
