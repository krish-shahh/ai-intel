TL;DR
Grok 4.5 went public today alongside GPT-5.6 in what multiple outlets called the first day all three major frontier labs have simultaneous public frontier models. Polymarket still prices Anthropic at 87% for "best AI model end of July" despite the launches, and OpenAI's 2026 IPO market collapsed toward zero after a confirmed 2027 delay.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] [[elonmusk]] Grok 4.5 is live (SpaceXAI, V9 foundation, 1.5T params, 500K context, $2/$6 per M tokens). SWE-Bench Pro: 64.7% — behind Fable 5 (80.4%) and Opus 4.8 (69.2%), but 4.2x more token-efficient per task than Opus 4.8. Musk positioned it as "Opus-class" while acknowledging it competes with "last year's Claude Opus." [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/373094/grok-4-5-elon-musk-claude-opus) · [ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/grok-4-5-public-launch-spacexai-july-2026) · [Crypto.news](https://crypto.news/elon-musk-unveils-grok-4-5-openai-ready-gpt-showdown/)

[[simonw]] had preview access for weeks and called it "very impressive."
