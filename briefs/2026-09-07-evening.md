## The signal

1. OpenAI publishes internal data on agent-driven research, paired with a chief scientist essay warning alignment hasn't kept pace
   What happened: OpenAI's "Research acceleration" post says its research org now logs 3.1 agent-workdays of coding-agent effort for every human workday, with the median researcher spending $600+/day on inference and the 90th percentile over $7,000/day; the same day, chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published "An Alien Mind," saying no lab, OpenAI included, has solved alignment and monitoring well enough to keep scaling at full speed, and that he expects voluntary slowdowns to become normal.
   Why it matters: this is the first hard internal number on how much AI research is already being done by AI, from the lab furthest along that curve, released alongside its own chief scientist saying safety work is behind the pace. Read together they're a rare admission against interest, not a launch pitch.
   Confidence: Confirmed

- Does: bundles 68 agents, 286 skills, 94 commands and runtime hooks into one "coordinated engineering system" for Claude Code and other agent harnesses — plan, test, implement, review, verify, remember, improve.
- Why now: trending on GitHub today, +1,485 stars in a day, part of the same wave of teams trying to impose structure on ad hoc agent usage.
