## The signal

1. **Anthropic publishes its open-weights position; Amodei calls for chip controls, anti-distillation rules, mandatory safety testing**
   What happened: [[darioamodei]] published "Our position on open-weights models" today, Anthropic's first direct statement since the company sat out the Nvidia-led open-weights letter. He says Anthropic never sought a ban; instead proposes three policies: tighter advanced chip/equipment exports to authoritarian governments, enforcement against industrial-scale distillation, and mandatory safety testing for all sufficiently capable models regardless of whether they are open or closed.
   Why it matters: The three asks land very differently in practice. Mandatory safety testing on open-weight releases creates a compliance burden that closed-weight labs with existing safety pipelines absorb far more easily than independent researchers or smaller labs. The post hit #1 on HN today with active skepticism in the thread about Anthropic's competitive motivations.
   Confidence: Confirmed

2. **China MOFCOM calls US Kimi K3 distillation sanction threats "AI hegemonism," warns of countermeasures**
   What happened: China's Ministry of Commerce issued a formal statement July 27 rejecting US threats to sanction Moonshot AI over alleged distillation of Anthropic's Fable to build Kimi K3. Beijing called the US position "lacking factual and legal grounds," warned it would take "all necessary measures," and counter-accused US AI firms of training on Chinese model outputs. The Bureau of Industry and Security is formally investigating Moonshot for distillation and banned chip access; no action taken yet. Anthropic previously disclosed that Moonshot generated over 3.4 million interactions with Claude through fraudulent accounts.
   Why it matters: This is the first formal government-to-government escalation over AI model IP. A BIS finding could restrict Moonshot's compute access and chill cross-border open-weight collaboration more broadly; analysts say the dispute risks derailing ongoing US-China AI safety talks.
   Confidence: Confirmed
