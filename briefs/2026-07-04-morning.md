TL;DR
Claude Sonnet 5 and Meituan's LongCat-2.0 (1.6T params, Chinese chips, MIT license) both shipped June 30; Meta's in-training Watermelon model claims GPT-5.5 parity and OpenAI is pitching a 5% US government equity stake.

## Models & research

- Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, now the default for Free/Pro plans and Claude Code, at $2/$10 per Mtok intro pricing through Aug 31 (then $3/$15). [[simonw]] flagged a practical catch: the same input generates ~30% more tokens than Sonnet 4.6, so per-task costs may exceed the headline price cut. [[model-releases]] [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/) · [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/30/claude-sonnet-5/)

- Meituan open-sourced LongCat-2.0 (June 30): 1.6T-param MoE with a native 1M-token context, MIT licensed, trained on 50k domestic Chinese ASICs and no Nvidia GPUs. It had been leading OpenRouter's coding charts for two months under the alias "Owl Alpha." SWE-Bench Pro score 59.5 beats GPT-5.5's 58.6. Weights are "coming soon" on HuggingFace; hosted API is live. [[model-releases]] [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meituan-open-sources-longcat-2-0-the-1-6t-near-frontier-agentic-coding-model-thats-been-leading-openrouter-trained-entirely-on-chinese-chips) · [SiliconAngle](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/30/chinas-meituan-open-sources-massive-longcat-2-0-ai-model-saying-trained-on-domestic-chips/)

- [[elonmusk]] announced June 28 that Grok 4.5 (1.5T-param V9 + Cursor coding data) entered private beta at SpaceX and Tesla. Musk claims performance "close to, perhaps exceeding" Claude Opus. No public API, no independent benchmarks exist. [[model-releases]] [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319314/20260629/grok-45-enters-private-beta-spacex-tesla-no-public-access-no-independent-benchmark.htm) · [LetsDatScience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/musk-says-grok-45-enters-private-beta-at-spacex-tesla-1840769e)

- Meta AI chief Alexandr Wang told staff July 2 that the in-training "Watermelon" model matched GPT-5.5 on internal benchmarks, using one order of magnitude more compute than the prior "Avocado" model. Caveat: OpenAI is already previewing GPT-5.6, so the target has moved. [[model-releases]] [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60264651/metas-upcoming-watermelon-ai-model-matches-openais-gpt-5-5-on-key-benchmarks-alexandr-wang-reportedly-tells-employees) · [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/07/03/meta-ai-chief-says-watermelon-model-has-caught-up-to-gpt-5-5-484022/)

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] updated to v2.1.197: Sonnet 5 is now the default model, new Manual permission mode ships on by default, background subagents run without blocking the main session, and transient 429 rate-limit errors retry automatically with backoff. [GitHub releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)

- Anthropic started blocking Chinese firms that used Singapore subsidiaries and VPNs to access Claude, following FT reporting that exposed Ant Financial and ByteDance doing this at scale. New policy: any company with 50%+ indirect Chinese ownership is banned globally; Anthropic is monitoring time zones and usage patterns to detect violations. [BanklessTimes](https://www.banklesstimes.com/articles/2026/07/03/anthropic-moves-to-block-chinese-firms-using-claude-via-offshore-workarounds/) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-targets-loopholes-used-by-chinese-firms-to-access-claude-ft-reports-4774998)
