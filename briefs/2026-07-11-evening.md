TL;DR
ICML 2026 closes today in Seoul — both outstanding paper awards went to diffusion model research. Meta Muse Image launched July 7 with a significant privacy default that has gone uncovered here: Instagram users were auto-enrolled for AI likeness use with no notification.

## Models & research

- [[ai-research]] ICML 2026 (July 6–11, Seoul, COEX) wraps today with 23,918 paper submissions — a conference record, more than double last year. Both Outstanding Paper Awards went to diffusion research: "The flexibility trap: Rethinking arbitrary order in diffusion language models" (Ni et al.) and "High-Accuracy Sampling for Diffusion Models and Log-Concave Distributions" (Chen et al.). Test of Time: Mnih et al.'s 2016 Asynchronous RL (A3C) paper. Workshops run today. [ICML awards blog](https://blog.icml.cc/2026/07/05/announcing-the-icml-2026-awards/) · [conference](https://icml.cc/)

## Tools & dev

- Meta launched Muse Image on July 7 — its first in-house image model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, currently ranked #2 on Arena's text-to-image leaderboard behind OpenAI. The main story: Meta defaulted every public adult Instagram account into the likeness-access pool, letting any user enter someone's handle and generate AI remixes of their face without notification or consent. CAA publicly demanded Meta switch to opt-in for artist likenesses; EU regulators flagged it; millions of users are scrambling to find the opt-out buried in settings. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/07/meta-rolls-out-muse-a-new-ai-image-generator/) · [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/07/caa-slams-meta-muse-image-ai-1236978047/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/07/meta-ai-muse-image.html)

- Fable 5 access on Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise plans expires **tomorrow, July 12 at 11:59 PM PT**. After the cutoff all Fable 5 tokens bill at usage credit rates: $10/$50 per million input/output. Anthropic says restoration to included subscription access is pending capacity. [Softonic](https://en.softonic.com/articles/anthropic-extends-free-claude-fable-5-access-through-july-12) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5)
