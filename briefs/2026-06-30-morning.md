TL;DR
Anthropic landed two big distribution moves overnight: Claude Opus 4.8 and Haiku 4.5 are now GA in Microsoft Azure Foundry, and California signed the first statewide AI deal giving all its agencies half-price Claude access. June 30 also closes GitHub Copilot's first usage-based billing month, with agentic devs posting 10-50x cost spikes.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Claude Opus 4.8 and Haiku 4.5 hit GA in Microsoft Foundry on Azure; teams get Azure-native identity, billing, and governance for Claude with NVIDIA GB300 Blackwell Ultra acceleration. "Hosted on Anthropic infrastructure" option stays in preview. [Azure blog](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/introducing-anthropics-claude-models-in-microsoft-foundry-bringing-frontier-intelligence-to-azure/) · [WindowsReport](https://windowsreport.com/claude-models-are-now-generally-available-in-microsoft-foundry-on-azure/)

## Tools & dev

- [[dev-tools]] June 30 closes GitHub Copilot's first full 30-day token billing cycle after the June 1 switch to usage-based pricing. Agentic users are posting 10-50x cost spikes -- bills moving from $29/mo to $750 and $50/mo to $3,000. Promotional credits expire September 1 and the flat-rate era is gone. [TechTimes Jun 29](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319340/20260629/github-copilot-billing-shock-confirmed-agentic-users-face-10x-cost-surge.htm) · [TechJournal](https://techjournal.org/github-copilot-token-billing-backlash)
