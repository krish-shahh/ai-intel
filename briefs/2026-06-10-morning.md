TL;DR
Anthropic dropped Claude Fable 5 (Mythos-class) on June 9 as its most capable publicly available model to date, while OpenAI quietly filed a confidential S-1 with the SEC and Apple's WWDC opened iOS 27 to Claude as a Siri backend.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] **Claude Fable 5 + Mythos 5** launched June 9. Fable 5 is the Mythos-class model in general availability with safety classifiers that route sensitive queries to Opus 4.8; Mythos 5 goes only to Project Glasswing cyberdefense partners. Pricing: $10/$50 per million tokens. Stripe reported compressing months of engineering into a single day in testing. Free on paid Claude plans through June 22. [Anthropic announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-released-claude-fable-5-its-most-powerful-model-publicly-days-after-warning-ai-is-getting-too-dangerous/)
- [[simonw]] posted first impressions of Fable 5 on June 9, noting software increasingly coming "on a tap" and Jevons' paradox driving his own demand up. [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/9/claude-fable-5/)
