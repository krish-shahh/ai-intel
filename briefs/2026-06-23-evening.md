TL;DR
Sakana AI dropped Fugu Ultra on June 22, a 7B orchestrator that beats Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 on SWE-Bench Pro by routing tasks across frontier models; GPT-5.6 launch window opened this week with June 25 flagged as the likely date.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Sakana AI launched Fugu and Fugu Ultra on June 22: a 7B RL conductor model that routes sub-tasks to a pool of Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, and Gemini 3.1 Pro, then synthesizes the results. Fugu Ultra scores 73.7 on SWE-Bench Pro (vs. Opus 4.8 at 69.2 and GPT-5.5 at 58.6), 95.5 on GPQA-D, through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint at $5/M input / $30/M output. Not available in EU/EEA. Fable 5 and Mythos are not in the pool because the US killed their access on June 12. [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/sakana-ais-fugu-orchestrates-multiple-llms-to-match-anthropics-fable-and-mythos-benchmarks/) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/22/sakana-ai-launches-sakana-fugu-an-orchestration-model-that-routes-tasks-across-a-swappable-pool-of-frontier-llms/)

- [[model-releases]] GPT-5.6 launch window opened Monday June 22 per OpenAI's internal timeline; a separate leak pegs June 25 as the drop date. Claimed features: 1.5M-token context (up from 1M in GPT-5.5), reasoning effort budget raised from 768 to 960, December 2025 knowledge cutoff. Still no official announcement or API string as of tonight. [TechTimes June 21](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318799/20260621/gpt-56-launch-window-starts-monday-alignment-fix-15m-token-context-inside.htm) · [Geeky Gadgets](https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/gpt-5-6-pro-leak-features/)
