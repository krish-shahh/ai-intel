[VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/openais-codex-update-lets-agents-build-interactive-enterprise-workspaces-via-sites-and-role-specific-plugins) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-upgrades-to-codex/)
