## Models & research

- OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 as three tiers on June 26: Sol (flagship, $5/$30 per 1M tokens), Terra (balanced, $2.50/$15), Luna (fast/cheap, $1/$6). Limited to ~20 US-vetted partners cleared by ONCD/OSTP; GA expected in weeks. Sol scored 88.8% on TerminalBench 2.1, just above Mythos 5 (88.0%). GPT-4.5 retired same day. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/26/openai-gpt-sol-terra-luna-trump) · [ExplainX benchmarks](https://explainx.ai/blog/gpt-5-6-release-date-features-benchmarks-2026)

- US Commerce Secretary Lutnick authorized Anthropic to release Mythos 5 to ~100 vetted companies and federal agencies on June 26, primarily members of Project Glasswing. Fable 5 is still locked down. Mythos had been offline since June 12 due to export/cybersecurity concerns over foreign national employees. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/26/tech/anthropic-mythos-release) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html) · [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/27/2026/us-releases-powerful-anthropic-model-mythos-to-some-us-companies)

- [[simonw]] wrote that GLM-5.2 from Z.ai is "probably the most powerful text-only open weights LLM" (June 17). The 753B MoE model (MIT licensed, ~39B active params, 1M token context) leads Artificial Analysis and beats GPT-5.5 on SWE-bench Pro (62.1 vs 58.6). Requires 256-512GB RAM to run locally. [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/jun/17/glm-52/) · [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/glm-5-2-is-the-new-leading-open-weights-model-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/z-ais-open-weights-glm-5-2-beats-gpt-5-5-on-multiple-long-horizon-coding-benchmarks-for-1-6th-the-cost)

- Gemini 3.5 Pro, promised by Sundar Pichai at Google I/O for "next month," is reportedly slipping to July for additional tuning. Gemini 3.5 Flash is already live. [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/is-google-delaying-gemini-35-pro-launch-to-july-for-further-testing)

- On benchmarks as of early June: Claude Opus 4.8 leads overall (67.9 on Artificial Analysis), GPT-5.5 holds coding edge (59.1% SWE-bench). Chinese models Qwen 3.7 Max and MiniMax 3 are matching Western flagships at a fraction of the cost. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)
