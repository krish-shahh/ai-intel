## The signal

1. Nvidia buys $3.5B in convertible bonds tied to MediaTek, its largest direct investment outside the US
   What happened: Nvidia will purchase bonds convertible into MediaTek shares, with MediaTek adopting Nvidia's NVLink Fusion interconnect to build custom accelerators and AI-vehicle silicon that plug into Nvidia's rack-scale systems.
   Why it matters: hyperscalers and labs (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic) are all building their own chips to cut Nvidia dependence; locking a major merchant chipmaker into NVLink Fusion is Nvidia's way of staying the connective tissue even as it cedes some silicon share.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/nvidia-to-invest-3-5-billion-in-chipmaker-mediatek) (independent) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/nvidias-3-5b-mediatek-bet-reveals-its-plan-for-tackling-big-techs-ai-chip-buildout/) (independent) · [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/09/01/2003863469) (independent)
