TL;DR
Hugging Face disclosed an autonomous AI agent breach of its dataset pipeline, logging 17K+ attacker actions in what appears to be the first documented end-to-end AI-driven intrusion at a major AI platform. Separately, Current AI raised $400M to build open, publicly governed AI infrastructure.

## Tools & dev

- [[agentic-ai]] Hugging Face disclosed July 20 that an autonomous AI agent breached its dataset processing pipeline, executing over 17,000 logged actions. Attacker exploited a remote-code dataset loader and a template-injection flaw, escalated to node-level access, and harvested internal cluster credentials. No public models, datasets, or Spaces were tampered with. Both the attack and the detection pipeline ran on AI agents, making this the clearest example yet of AI-vs-AI security in production. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hugging-face-breach-autonomous-ai-agent-system-internal-datasets-credentials/) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/07/20/hugging-face-breached-by-autonomous-ai-agent/)

- EU adopted two binding DMA decisions requiring Google to give rival AI assistants the same Android access as Gemini: mic, camera, screen, wake-word while display is off, and the ability to drive other apps. Must ship in Android 18 by August 2027. A separate order requires Google to share Search data with competing AI services on FRAND terms starting January 2027. [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/20/eu-orders-google-open-android-search-data-ai-services/)
