## The signal

1. Anthropic reports state-linked actors used Claude for weapons and surveillance work
   What happened: Anthropic's September threat intelligence report says operators tied to China, Iran, Russia, and Yemen used Claude to advance weapons systems, including an anti-torpedo system for China's navy and missile-related work by Houthi operators in Yemen, plus surveillance efforts including tracking Uyghurs; Anthropic says its safeguards blocked many but not all requests and found no evidence of an operational weapon being fielded.
   Why it matters: it's the most detailed documented case yet of frontier models being used across the full state-actor threat spectrum (weapons, cyber, surveillance, influence ops), not just chat-based misuse, and raises the bar for what pre-deployment safeguards need to catch.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026) (primary) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/anthropic-says-us-adversaries-aimed-claude-at-weapons-research) (independent)

2. A single attacker ran hundreds of Codex-based AI agents to breach 395 organizations in under a day
   What happened: a Russian-speaking threat actor combined OpenAI's Codex harness with a DeepSeek model, plus Mimikatz, Certipy, Rubeus, and Impacket, to exploit two PaperCut NG/MF CVEs, going from an empty workspace to code execution in under 4 hours and breaching 11 organizations in 26 seconds once at full speed, compromising 440+ servers across 395 organizations in 48 countries.
   Why it matters: this is the concrete scenario behind the Aug 27 OpenAI/Anthropic/Microsoft joint warning about a "limited window" before AI-enabled cyberattacks scale, now demonstrated at real scale. Agentic tooling is collapsing the time between vulnerability disclosure and mass exploitation.
   Confidence: Reported
   Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/papercut-attacker-uses-hundreds-of-ai.html) (independent) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ai-powered-attack-exploited-papercut-flaws-to-hack-395-organizations/) (independent) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/11/ai-agents-papercut-ng-mf-attack-campaign/) (independent)
