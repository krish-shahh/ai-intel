## Other

- JADEPUFFER (Sysdig, July 1): first fully autonomous AI ransomware operation. An LLM agent exploited CVE-2025-3248 (unauth RCE in Langflow), pivoted, escalated privileges, and encrypted 1,342 Nacos service configs — then discarded the key, leaving data unrecoverable even if the ransom was paid. Tell: LLM-generated throwaways contain natural-language commentary explaining each step; human operators don't annotate disposable one-liners. Skill floor for running ransomware just dropped to the cost of running an agent. [[agentic-ai]] [Sysdig](https://www.sysdig.com/blog/jadepuffer-agentic-ransomware-for-automated-database-extortion) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/jadepuffer-ransomware-used-ai-agent-to-automate-entire-attack/)
