TL;DR
Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are still offline globally under a US export control order with no restore date. Claude Sonnet 4 and Opus 4 API endpoints retire tomorrow at 9AM PT with no auto-failover.

## Models & research

- Fable 5 and Mythos 5 went offline June 12 after a US government export directive, citing a claimed jailbreak where the model reads a codebase to surface software flaws; Anthropic calls it a "misunderstanding," is complying while pushing back publicly, and has no ETA for restoration -- all other Claude models are unaffected. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/12/anthropics-safety-warnings-may-have-just-backfired-the-government-has-pulled-the-plug-on-its-most-powerful-ai/) · [Anthropic statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-blocks-all-public-access-to-claude-fable-5-mythos-5-following-us-government-order-what-enterprises-should-do)
- Moonshot AI dropped Kimi K2.7-Code on June 12: open-weight 1T-param MoE (32B active, 384 experts), 256K context window, Modified MIT license; self-reported +21.8% on its Kimi Code Bench v2 and beats Claude Opus 4.8 on MCPMark Verified (81.1% vs 76.4%) with ~30% fewer reasoning tokens; no independent third-party numbers yet. [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/12/moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k2-7-code-a-coding-model-reporting-21-8-on-kimi-code-bench-v2-over-k2-6/)
