TL;DR
Google's Gemini CLI cutoff takes effect today, locking out all free and paid non-enterprise users. OpenAI released its Deployment Simulation methodology for pre-release model behavioral testing.

## Tools & dev

- [[dev-tools]] Gemini CLI stops serving free, Pro, and Ultra users today with no grace period; the replacement is Antigravity CLI, a closed-source Go rewrite that launched without the original's source code. Google accepted 6,000+ community PRs over roughly a year, then moved the tool to enterprise-only and shipped a closed replacement. Enterprise Gemini Code Assist licenses are unaffected. [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/20/bye-bye-gemini-cli-google-nudges-devs-toward-antigravity/5243605) · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48196867)

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] OpenAI published Deployment Simulation (Jun 16): strips assistant replies from 1.3M de-identified past conversations and replays the prompts through a candidate model before release to catch behavioral drift, novel misalignment, and reward hacking, including agentic tool-call scenarios. [openai.com](https://openai.com/index/deployment-simulation/) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/16/openai-deployment-simulation/)
