TL;DR
Anthropic had the biggest 48 hours of its year: Fable 5 + Mythos 5 came back globally (export controls lifted June 30, access restored July 1), and Claude Sonnet 5 shipped as the new default across Claude Code and all user tiers. Meanwhile GPT-5.6 Sol is still gated to ~20 partner orgs with no GA date, and Gemini 3.5 Pro keeps slipping.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are back globally as of July 1, ending a 19-day suspension triggered by US export controls applied on June 12. Pro/Max/Team users get up to 50% of weekly usage limits free through July 7, then usage credits. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/1/us-lifts-restrictions-on-powerful-ai-models-fable-mythos-anthropic-says) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-says-trump-admin-has-lifted-export-controls-on-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5.html)

- [[model-releases]] Claude Sonnet 5 (released June 30) is now the default model for all Claude users and the new default in [[claude-code]], with a 1M-token context window and introductory pricing of $2/$10 per Mtok through August 31. Described as the most agentic Sonnet yet, it runs close to Opus 4.8 on many tasks at a fraction of the cost. [AI Business](https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/restrictions-fable-5-mythos-5-lifted-anthropic-launches-sonnet-5)

- [[model-releases]] GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna preview (announced June 26) is still locked to roughly 20 government-cleared orgs. Sol ($5/$30 per Mtok) targets hard reasoning and agentic coding; Terra ($2.50/$15) is the everyday workhorse; Luna ($1/$6) is lowest-cost. OpenAI says Sol will launch on Cerebras at up to 750 tokens/sec in July, with broad GA expected "in coming weeks." [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov/)

- [[model-releases]] Gemini 3.5 Pro slipped from June to July GA. Standout specs: 2M-token context, Deep Think reasoning (Ultra tier only). The delay coincides with four senior Google researchers departing for OpenAI and Anthropic, including Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319318/20260629/gemini-35-pro-cleared-july-launch-fable-5-nears-return-gpt-56-stays-locked.htm) · [Bind AI](https://blog.getbind.co/gemini-3-5-pro-slips-to-july-and-four-senior-google-researchers-just-left-for-anthropic/)

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] Latest Claude Code update ships session reliability overhaul: long-running agent sessions now survive process restarts on all platforms; stalled agents get labeled "Needs attention"; completed rows no longer flip state. Also adds org default models, clickable file attachments, and a 25% token reduction in the /code-review workflow. [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)

- [[agentic-ai]] [[emollick]] cited new OpenAI internal data showing the chatbot era is functionally over inside the company: agentic task completion is spreading beyond engineering to knowledge work broadly. "The chatbot era is over, and agentic systems are coming to tasks beyond engineering." [X](https://x.com/emollick/status/2070171580030656744)

- [[dev-tools]] The CREATE AI Act, a rare bipartisan bill, is moving through Congress. It aims to establish federal standards for AI deployment across government and industry. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/01/create-ai-act-bipartisan-deserves-to-pass-cognizant/)

- [[steipete]] "Loop engineering" framing — Peter Steinberger's June 8 post ("you shouldn't be prompting coding agents anymore, you should be designing loops that prompt your agents") hit 6.5M views on X and is still circulating widely among agentic dev communities. [ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/loop-engineering-coding-agents-claude-code-guide-2026)
