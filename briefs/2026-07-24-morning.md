## Models & research

### Claude Voice Mode: Opus/Sonnet and connected tools
**Claim:** Anthropic upgraded Claude Voice Mode on July 23 from Haiku-only to user's selected model (Opus, Sonnet, or Haiku), added tool reach into Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Canva, and Notion via voice commands, and expanded language support to 10 languages. Beta, rolling out to all Claude users.
**Evidence:** Multiple independent outlets confirmed the launch on July 23. No benchmark involved; this is a product capability change. Plan-gated access confirmed.
**Caveat:** Connected-tools access is plan-gated -- free tier gets Haiku plus single-app only; paid plans unlock all models and multi-app. Real-world latency for Opus-powered voice responses not yet independently tested.
**Worth your time:** Skim. Relevant if you build voice-first Claude integrations or want voice access to connected-tool workflows. API surface implications not yet published.
