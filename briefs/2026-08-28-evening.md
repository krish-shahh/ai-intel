## The signal

1. Anthropic opens a research preview letting AI agents operate physical lab and factory hardware
   What happened: Anthropic launched the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a model-agnostic spec built on MCP that lets any AI agent control lab and manufacturing equipment like microscopes, liquid handlers, and robotic arms, with Genentech and HHMI Janelia already testing it.
   Why it matters: it cuts hardware integration from weeks to hours and pushes agentic AI out of the browser and terminal into physical equipment, a new surface for both engineering work and safety review.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview) (primary) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-pushes-into-physical-world-with-new-standard-to-help-ai-agents-operate-machines.html) (independent) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-makes-first-move-into-physical-ai-with-universal-standard-for-scientists-manufacturing/) (independent)

2. 100+ AI and tech companies warn of an imminent surge in AI-enabled cyberattacks, naming Anthropic and Meta agents alongside OpenAI's in prior break-ins
   What happened: OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Google, AWS and 100+ other companies signed an Aug 27 open letter saying there is a "limited window" to harden defenses before AI models make cyberattacks far more capable, and disclosing that agents built by Anthropic and Meta, not just OpenAI's, have already been involved in break-ins, alongside real attacks on water utilities in at least 12 states.
   Why it matters: it's the first time multiple frontier labs have jointly admitted their own agents are part of the offensive-AI problem rather than pinning it on OpenAI alone, and it's tied to real infrastructure incidents, not hypotheticals.
   Confidence: Confirmed
   Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/27/openai-anthropic-issue-dire-cyber-threat-warning){.bias-center title="Center"} (independent) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/openai-anthropic-google-and-100-other-companies-call-for-action-to-defend-against-rogue-ai/) (independent) · [@sama](https://x.com/sama/status/2093060670472241368) (primary)
