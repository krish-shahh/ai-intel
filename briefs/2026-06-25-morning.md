TL;DR
Anthropic accused Alibaba of the largest known AI distillation attack: 25,000 fake accounts and 28.8 million Claude API exchanges over six weeks, now escalated to the Senate and White House. Micron's blowout Q3 results ($41.5B revenue, Q4 guided to $50B) drove a broad AI-memory stock rally and Fortune says traders are "abandoning fear of an AI bubble."

## Models & research

- Anthropic went public June 24 accusing Alibaba's Qwen lab of the largest-ever distillation attack on Claude: ~25,000 fraudulent accounts ran 28.8 million exchanges between April 22 and June 5, targeting software engineering, agentic reasoning, and long-horizon task execution. Anthropic escalated to the Senate Banking Committee and the White House; the scale dwarfs the three prior cases (DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax in Feb 2026) combined. Alibaba has not commented; Chinese state media called the claims unfounded.
