TL;DR
Anthropic's June Economic Index landed today: half of 9,700 surveyed Claude users say AI can already handle 50%+ of their job. OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna remain gated to ~20 US-approved orgs under the new frontier AI review process.

## Models & research

- Anthropic published the June Economic Index "Cadences" report today: among ~9,700 surveyed Claude users, 50% say AI can handle half or more of their job tasks today, 4% say it could replace their entire role now. Work queries taper on weekends, high-income tasks cluster at night. [[model-releases]] [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report) · [GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260629-anthropic-economic-report)

- OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 as three tiers on June 26 (Sol flagship at $5/$30 per 1M tokens, Terra balanced at $2.50/$15, Luna cheap at $1/$6) but limited access to ~20 trusted partners at the US government's request under Trump's June 2 frontier AI EO; broad rollout expected in coming weeks.
