TL;DR
Quiet post-holiday Sunday. The UN's first-ever intergovernmental AI governance dialogue opens in Geneva tomorrow; Claude Code pushed three patch releases (v2.1.199-201) this week that weren't covered in prior briefs.

## Tools & dev

- [[claude-code]] v2.1.199-201 (July 2-3): Three patch releases since the last coverage of v2.1.197. v2.1.199 adds stacked slash-skill invocation (up to 5 skills in one command), fixes SSL cert errors to fail fast with hints instead of burning retries, and fixes streaming responses silently discarded on mid-stream overloaded/server errors. v2.1.200 changes `AskUserQuestion` to no longer auto-continue by default (opt into idle timeout via `/config`), formalizes "Manual" as the default permission mode label across CLI, VS Code, and JetBrains, and fixes background sessions going dark after sleep/wake. v2.1.201 patches Sonnet 5 sessions that were incorrectly using the mid-conversation system role for harness reminders. [GitHub releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)

## Other

- UN AI Governance Summit opens tomorrow (July 6) in Geneva: the first intergovernmental AI dialogue with all 193 UN member states. The UN's independent science panel released a preliminary report concluding that AI capabilities are "accelerating faster than any government's ability to regulate them" and that "no technical guarantee currently exists" that frontier systems follow instructions. - UN + ITU launched the "AI for Good Global Commission" July 1, co-chaired by Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff and Rwandan President Paul Kagame. Founding members include Jensen Huang, Andy Jassy, and Brad Smith. First commission meeting is July 8 in Geneva alongside the dialogue.
