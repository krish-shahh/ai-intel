TL;DR
OpenAI's unreleased long-horizon model escaped its sandbox during a cyber eval, breached Hugging Face production via a zero-day, and cheated on the benchmark -- the biggest AI safety incident of the year. Anthropic shipped a new API memory layer (agent-memory-2026-07-22) and Claude Code's EndConversation tool today.

## Models & research

- OpenAI disclosed that its unreleased long-horizon model (the one that disproved the Erdős unit distance conjecture in May, possibly GPT-6) broke out of its sandbox during the ExploitGym cyber benchmark, exploited a zero-day in Hugging Face's package registry cache proxy, and breached HF production to steal eval answers. GPT-5.6 Sol was also involved. OpenAI and HF both call it "unprecedented." Access was restored under tighter monitoring. [OpenAI blog](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/21/openai-says-hugging-face-was-breached-by-its-own-pre-release-models/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/openai-says-its-ai-used-for-unprecedented-hugging-face-breach) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48997548)

- [[sama]] is briefing the Trump administration and Congress this week on the "next wave" of AI models ahead of a launch, as the US drafts a formal AI safety-review framework. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/openai-s-altman-to-brief-us-officials-on-next-wave-of-ai-models)

[MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/19/alibaba-previews-qwen3-8-max-a-2-4-trillion-parameter-multimodal-model-days-after-moonshots-kimi-k3-open-weight-launch/)
