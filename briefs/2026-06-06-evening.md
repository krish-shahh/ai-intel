TL;DR
Anthropic filed a confidential S-1 with the SEC on June 1 at a $965B valuation. Claude Opus 4.8, out May 28, is now #1 on all major benchmarks: 96.7% on USAMO 2026 and 88.6% on SWE-bench Verified.

## Models & research

- [[model-releases]] Claude Opus 4.8 launched May 28 at $5/$25 per MTok. New in this release: Dynamic Workflows for parallel agentic tasks, Effort Control for tuning reasoning depth, and Fast Mode now 3x cheaper than Opus 4.7. It scores 96.7% on USAMO 2026 (a +27 pt jump in one cycle) and 88.6% on SWE-bench Verified, putting it at #1 overall on Artificial Analysis. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-8) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-releases-opus-4-8-with-new-dynamic-workflow-tool/)

- [[model-releases]] Microsoft dropped 7 new MAI models at Build 2026 on June 2. MAI-Thinking-1 is a 35B MoE with a 256K context window and 53% on SWE Bench Pro; MAI-Code-1-Flash handles code generation. All trained from scratch with no OpenAI distillation. [[simonw]] covered it same day. [Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/building-a-hillclimbing-machine-launching-seven-new-mai-models/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/microsoft-unveils-new-ai-models-lessen-reliance-on-openai-lower-costs.html) · [Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/2/microsofts-new-models/)
