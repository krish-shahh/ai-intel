TL;DR
Claude Sonnet 5 launched June 30 as the default agentic model and Fable 5 was fully restored July 1 after a 19-day export-control suspension; Meituan's LongCat-2.0, a 1.6T open-source model trained entirely on Chinese chips, quietly topped OpenRouter before its June 30 public reveal. (Note: covers June 30-July 5 — gap since last run on June 29.)

## Models & research

- Claude Sonnet 5 launched June 30: 63.2% SWE-bench Pro, 80.4% TerminalBench 2.1 (beats Opus 4.8's 74.6%), 1M context, $2/$10 MTok intro pricing through Aug 31; default for Free/Pro claude.ai users as of July 1. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/)

- Fable 5 and Mythos 5 fully restored July 1 globally after 19-day suspension triggered by an Amazon researcher jailbreak that coaxed the model into generating exploit code. Anthropic added a classifier blocking that prompt vector >99% of attempts; Pro/Max/Team get Fable 5 up to 50% of weekly limit through July 7, then via usage credits. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/01/claude-fable-5-cleared-to-return-as-us-lifts-anthropics-export-control-restriction/) · [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463808)

- Meituan open-sourced LongCat-2.0 on June 30: 1.6T MoE, MIT license, first model at this scale trained entirely on 50,000 domestic Chinese ASICs with no NVIDIA. Scored 59.5% SWE-bench Pro (vs. GPT-5.5's 58.6%). Had been quietly topping OpenRouter under alias "Owl Alpha." Full weights not yet posted on Hugging Face. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meituan-open-sources-longcat-2-0-the-1-6t-near-frontier-agentic-coding-model-thats-been-leading-openrouter-trained-entirely-on-chinese-chips) · [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-2.0)

- [[elonmusk]] confirmed June 28 that Grok 4.5 (1.5T params, V9 architecture, supplemental Cursor training data) is in private beta at SpaceX and Tesla. Musk claims "close to or exceeding Opus" but no independent benchmark exists and there is no public API. xAI says it targets a new model monthly for the rest of 2026. [Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/grok-4-5-private-beta-spacex-tesla/)

- Meta AI chief Alexandr Wang told staff internally that Watermelon, the next model after Avocado and using 10x more compute, now matches GPT-5.5 on current evals. No benchmarks named, no third-party confirmation. [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60264651/metas-upcoming-watermelon-ai-model-matches-openais-gpt-5-5-on-key-benchmarks-alexandr-wang-reportedly-tells-employees)

- GPT-5.6 Sol coming to Cerebras at up to 750 tok/sec in July; currently limited to select customers as capacity expands. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)
