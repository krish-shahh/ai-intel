Broadcom designs custom AI accelerators (ASICs) for hyperscalers -- including Google's TPUs and reported programs with Meta and OpenAI -- as an alternative to buying merchant GPUs, plus the Tomahawk/Jericho switch silicon that underpins scale-out networking inside AI datacenters. Its AI-related revenue has become a major growth driver alongside its enterprise software business (VMware). CEO Hock Tan has framed custom silicon as the natural next step once a hyperscaler's AI workload is large enough to justify the design cost.
