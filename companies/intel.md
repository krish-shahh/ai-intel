Intel is a US semiconductor manufacturer and foundry operator, central to efforts to bring advanced chip and memory production back onshore, including its Ohio fab buildout. It competes with TSMC in foundry services and with AMD and NVIDIA in compute, and is increasingly positioning its US fabs as capacity other chipmakers can lease into or partner around.
