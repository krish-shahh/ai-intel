MediaTek is a Taiwanese fabless chipmaker best known for smartphone SoCs (Dimensity) that is expanding into AI edge and datacenter silicon, including custom accelerators built with Nvidia's NVLink Fusion interconnect.

## Recent

- **2026-08-31** Nvidia agreed to invest $3.5B via convertible bonds into MediaTek shares, Nvidia's largest direct investment outside the US. MediaTek will adopt NVLink Fusion to build custom accelerators and AI-vehicle silicon that plug into Nvidia's rack-scale systems.
