Micron is the largest US-based memory maker and a growing HBM supplier, competing with SK Hynix and Samsung for the AI accelerator memory market while also supplying the conventional DRAM and NAND that datacenters consume at scale. AI-driven demand has pulled memory pricing and capacity into a tight cycle, making Micron's earnings calls a regular source of read-through on the broader memory market. It has also announced large US fab expansions tied to CHIPS Act incentives.
