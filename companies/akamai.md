Akamai is a content-delivery and edge-computing network that has been pushing into AI compute capacity supply, offering CPU-based compute (rather than GPUs) to AI labs building out inference and research infrastructure.

## Recent

- **2026-09-25** Signed an $11.6B, seven-year deal to supply Anthropic with CPU compute capacity, its largest deal ever and more than 6x a $1.8B pact from May; Akamai plans to spend $5.5B building out the capacity and issued Anthropic a warrant convertible into up to ~5% of Akamai's own stock, vesting as Anthropic spends more.
