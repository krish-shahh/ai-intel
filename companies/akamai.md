Akamai is a content-delivery and edge-computing network that has been pushing into AI compute capacity supply, offering CPU-based compute (rather than GPUs) to AI labs building out inference and research infrastructure.

## Recent

- **2026-09-27** Akamai's own investor filing breaks out the warrant vesting schedule: about 2% of Akamai's common stock vests with the initial $11.6B commitment, with the remaining ~3% vesting as Anthropic buys through an additional $9B of expansion (each $3B tranche vesting roughly 1%). Akamai projects $150-300M of revenue from the deal in 2027, ramping to an annualized pace of about $1.7B by the end of 2028. [Akamai IR](https://www.ir.akamai.com/news-releases/news-release-details/akamai-announces-116-billion-multi-year-agreement-anthropic) (primary)

- **2026-09-25** Signed an $11.6B, seven-year deal to supply Anthropic with CPU compute capacity, its largest deal ever and more than 6x a $1.8B pact from May; Akamai plans to spend $5.5B building out the capacity and issued Anthropic a warrant convertible into up to ~5% of Akamai's own stock, vesting as Anthropic spends more. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropic-to-pay-akamai-11-6-billion-over-seven-years-in-cloud-deal/) (independent) · [Akamai](https://www.akamai.com/newsroom/press-release/akamai-announces-11-6-billion-multi-year-agreement-with-anthropic-to-support-growing-demand) (primary)
