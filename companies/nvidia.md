NVIDIA designs the GPUs and AI accelerators that dominate model training and inference, and has extended that position into networking (via its Mellanox/InfiniBand and NVLink lines) and full-rack systems (NVL72, Grace Blackwell). Its data-center segment now accounts for the large majority of revenue, driven by hyperscaler capex on AI infrastructure. CEO Jensen Huang (see [[jensenhuang]]) sets the annual GPU architecture cadence that the rest of the compute stack largely builds around.
