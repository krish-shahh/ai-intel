ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) is China's leading DRAM maker, based in Hefei and founded in 2016. It listed on Shanghai's STAR Market in July 2026 and is the domestic answer to Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron as Beijing pushes for memory self-sufficiency, though it still trails the leaders on process node and HBM capability.

## Recent

- **2026-09-20** Announced its fifth-generation (G5) DRAM platform entered mass production at an 11.95nm active-area half-pitch, with two new 24Gb LPDDR5X parts and roughly 50% more dies per wafer than the prior generation, narrowing the process gap with [[sk-hynix]] and [[micron]] on legacy-node DRAM.
