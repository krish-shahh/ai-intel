GE Vernova makes the gas turbines, grid equipment, and power generation hardware that utilities and hyperscalers are ordering to meet AI datacenter electricity demand, which has driven turbine order backlogs out several years. It sits upstream of the datacenter itself, at the point where new electricity generation and transmission capacity gets built to feed the grid a datacenter draws from -- a frequent hard constraint on how fast new AI capacity can come online.
