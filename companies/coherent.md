Coherent makes the optical transceivers and components that move data between racks and buildings inside AI datacenters, a layer that has become a bottleneck as cluster sizes outgrow copper interconnects. Demand for higher-speed optics (800G and beyond) tracks directly with hyperscaler AI cluster buildouts, and it competes with vendors like Lumentum and innovations in co-packaged optics from the larger silicon players.
