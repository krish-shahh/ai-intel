AMD is the primary alternative to NVIDIA in AI accelerators, with its Instinct MI-series GPUs and EPYC server CPUs competing for datacenter compute spend. It has pushed hard on open software (ROCm) to close the gap with CUDA's ecosystem lock-in, and has landed large multi-year supply agreements with hyperscalers and AI labs to diversify their GPU sourcing away from a single vendor. CEO Lisa Su has positioned AMD as the credible second source for frontier-scale training and inference clusters.
