SK Hynix is the leading supplier of High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), the stacked-DRAM technology that sits next to every modern AI accelerator and is frequently the binding constraint on how many GPUs a vendor can actually ship. It has been NVIDIA's primary HBM partner through successive HBM3/HBM3E/HBM4 generations, and its capacity and yield updates are closely watched as a leading indicator for AI accelerator supply. Samsung and Micron compete for the same HBM sockets.
