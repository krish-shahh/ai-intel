Vertiv supplies the power and thermal infrastructure inside AI datacenters -- UPS systems, power distribution, and increasingly liquid cooling (CDUs, rear-door heat exchangers) needed as GPU racks outgrow air cooling. Its order backlog and guidance are treated as a direct signal of hyperscaler datacenter buildout pace, since it sits right where compute demand turns into physical construction spend. Liquid cooling adoption, driven by NVIDIA's Grace Blackwell-generation power density, has become its fastest-growing product line.
